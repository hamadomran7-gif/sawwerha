Maximum domestic worker recruitment price in Kuwait

CategoryMaximum (KWD)Includes
Asia750Air ticket and procedures
Africa575Air ticket and procedures
Private passport350Ticket not stated

Set by Commerce Minister Decision 2/2024 on 9 January 2024, amending Decision 103/2022. The cap was KWD 990 in 2018, 890 in February 2021, then 850 for the Philippines and 700 for India, Sri Lanka and Nepal excluding the ticket in August 2022. Some articles still cite 990 in 2025, but we found no decision raising the cap after 2/2024.

New residence fees for domestic workers

{{table:kw-recruit-residence lang=en}}

The new regulation took effect on 23 December 2025. Temporary residence for a domestic worker is KWD 5 a month. The fee for an expat family's 5th worker has not been published; the table assumes 500, the same as the 4th.

The office's 6-month guarantee

Under Article 6 of Ministerial Decision 2194/2016, the office refunds what was paid and takes the worker back within 6 months of receipt, in cases including refusal to work and absence. If the worker leaves before 6 months, the full amount is refunded with the return ticket.

How much is a housemaid's salary in Kuwait?

The legal minimum is KWD 75 a month (Law 68/2015). The Philippines requires KWD 120 (USD 400), offices raised Filipina contracts to KWD 150 by agreement in 2025, and Ethiopian workers get about KWD 100 when new and 110 with experience.

Examples:
Asian worker for a Kuwaiti family with fewer than 3 workers, salary 120: 750 + 10 + 120 × 12 = KWD 2,200 in the first year, or 2,560 at a salary of 150.
Asian worker as an expat family's second worker, salary 120: 750 + 50 + 1,440 = KWD 2,240; as the third, the residence fee is 400.

If you are an expat, also see your family residence fees in Kuwait, and compare with Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
