Pay for working on a holiday

All hours worked on holidays and Eids count as overtime (Article 107(3)), paid at the actual hourly wage plus 50% of the basic hourly wage on top of the holiday pay already in your salary. With the worker's consent, paid compensatory leave of at least an hour and a half per hour can replace the cash. We calculate the hourly wage as monthly pay ÷ 30 ÷ 8.

Example: basic SAR 6,000 and actual SAR 8,000, working 8 hours on Eid: 8,000 ÷ 240 = 33.33, and 50% × 6,000 ÷ 240 = 12.50, so each hour is 45.83 and you are owed SAR 366.67.

Holidays that fall on the weekly rest day

The worker gets a similar day before or after it (Article 24 of the regulations), so if the Eid al-Fitr 2027 holiday includes Friday 12 March, a day in lieu is due. National Day or Founding Day inside an Eid holiday is not compensated. A holiday inside annual leave extends the leave by the same number of days.

Government sector

Civil servants have much longer holidays (about two weeks for each Eid), and Labour Law employees of government bodies get 4 to 5 working days per Eid under Council of Ministers Resolution 532. No private-sector public holidays remain in 2026.

Eid dates are expected dates by the Umm al-Qura calendar; the Ministry of Human Resources announces them before each Eid.
