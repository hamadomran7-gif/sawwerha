What a final settlement in Kuwait includes

ItemHow it is calculatedArticle
End-of-service indemnity15 days per year for the first 5 years then a month, capped at 18 months, on the last full wage; on resignation nothing before 3 years, half to 5, two thirds to 1051–53
Leave payFull wage ÷ 26 × unused days70 and 71–74
Notice3 months for monthly-paid workers, on the full wage44
Unpaid salaryFull wage ÷ 30 × unpaid days—

Example: A full wage of KWD 800, 6 years and 10 months ended by resignation, and 10 leave days: the full indemnity is 3,774.36 and you get two thirds, 2,516.24, plus 307.69 leave pay, a total of KWD 2,823.93.

Notice pay covers the unserved days: the employer pays it when ending the contract without full notice, and it is deducted from a worker who leaves without it. For detail use the end-of-service calculator and the leave calculator.
