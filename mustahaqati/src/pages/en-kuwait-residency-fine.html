Calculate the fine

Fines under Kuwait's Residency Law 114/2024

ViolationArticleDaily fineMaximum
Residence expired or renewal refused, and not leaving132 in the first month, then 41,200
Domestic workers132600
Residence not obtained after entry92 then 4 (domestic 2)1,200 (domestic 600)
Temporary residence or departure notice exceeded122 then 41,200
Visit or entry visa exceeded11102,000
Leaving a job, then a new residence granted17, 18 and 202 then 41,200
Newborn registered after 4 months62 in the first month, then 42,000

Amounts are in Kuwaiti dinars and are settlement fines paid to close the violation. The decree-law took effect on 5 January 2025 and its executive regulations on 23 December 2025, with no change to the amounts. Related fees: temporary residence KD 10 a month (KD 5 for domestic workers) and a departure notice KD 10 a month.

Examples:
Residence expired on 1 August 2026, leaving on 15 October (75 days): 30 × 2 + 45 × 4 = KD 240.
A domestic worker in the same case: 75 × 2 = KD 150.
Visit visa expired on 1 August, leaving on 15 October: 75 × 10 = KD 750.
The residence fine reaches its KD 1,200 cap on day 315, and the visit fine its KD 2,000 cap on day 200.

We assume the "first month" is 30 days starting the day after the residence expires, and no grace period is published. If the violator does not settle by paying, the case is referred and deportation is possible. To work out what you are owed when leaving a job, use the Kuwait indemnity calculator.
