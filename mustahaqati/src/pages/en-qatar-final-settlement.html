What a final settlement in Qatar includes

ItemHow it is calculatedArticle
End-of-service gratuity21 days of the last basic wage per year after one year, no cut for resignation54 and 61
Leave payBasic ÷ 30 × unused days79–81
NoticeOne month up to two years of service, then two, on the basic wage49
Unpaid salaryFull wage ÷ 30 × unpaid days—

Example: Basic 6,000 and allowances 3,000, 6 years and 10 months ended by the employer with 30 of 60 notice days served, 15 leave days and 10 unpaid days: gratuity 28,700, leave 3,000, notice 6,000 and salary 3,000, a total of QAR 40,700.

Notice pay covers the unserved days: the employer pays it when ending the contract without full notice, and it is deducted from a worker who leaves without it. For detail use the end-of-service calculator and the leave calculator.
