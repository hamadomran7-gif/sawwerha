Calculate your renewal fees

Wife, children, parents and anyone on your sponsorship. The fee is the same for all.
Iqama fee of SAR 500 a year for a family member above the legal age. Those listed on your iqama don't pay it.

The official iqama fee is SAR 500 a year; SAR 650 (500 plus 150 in other charges) is what is commonly charged, so check the amount shown in Absher or Muqeem. Optional platform service fees are not included.

Who pays what?

  • Employer: the expat levy, the work permit fee, the worker's iqama fee and renewal, and their late fines (Article 40 of the Labor Law). These may not be charged to the worker.
  • Head of family: the dependent fee, the iqama fee for any family member with their own iqama, and the family's exit and re-entry visas.

Example: wife and 3 children for 12 months: 4 × 400 × 12 = SAR 19,200 (SAR 4,800 for 3 months). The worker's own renewal for 12 months with more expats than Saudis: 9,600 + 100 + 650 = SAR 10,350, paid by the employer.
