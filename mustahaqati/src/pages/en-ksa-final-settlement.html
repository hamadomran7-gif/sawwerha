What goes into a Saudi final settlement

ItemHow it is calculatedArticle
End-of-service awardHalf a month per year for the first five years, then a month, reduced on resignation by length of service84, 85
Unused leaveDaily wage (wage ÷ 30) × days111
Notice compensationWage for the unserved days of 60 (employer) or 30 (worker)75, 76
Unpaid salaryDaily wage × days worked but not paid—

Example: wage SAR 9,000 and 8 years of service ended by the employer of an open-ended contract with 30 days' notice instead of 60, and 21 days of unused leave: award 49,500, leave pay 6,300 and 30 days of notice 9,000, a total of SAR 64,800.
