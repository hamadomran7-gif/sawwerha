Work out your overtime pay

Basic only: the law calculates overtime on it.

Overtime rules in Qatar

ItemRule
Normal hours8 a day and 48 a week (Article 73)
Ramadan6 a day and 36 a week, not limited to Muslims
Daily limit10 actual hours including overtime (Article 74)
Daytime overtimeBasic pay + at least 25%
Night overtime+ 50% between 9 pm and 3 am, except shift workers
Rest day (normally Friday)A substitute rest day, or basic pay + at least 150% (Article 75)
Public holidaysArticle 74 applies: + 25%, or + 50% at night
ExcludedSenior posts holding the employer's authority (Article 76)

Example: Basic salary QAR 6,000, so the hourly wage is 6,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = QAR 25. Ten daytime overtime hours: 10 × 25 × 1.25 = 312.50; four hours on Friday without a substitute day: 4 × 25 × 2.5 = 250. Total QAR 562.50; with a substitute day, 312.50 plus a day off.

The night window in the Arabic text of Article 74 is 9 pm to 3 am; some English guides extend it to 6 am. Law 9 of 2026 did not change the working-hours and overtime articles.
