Calculate your maternity pay

To work out the last day and return date.
Up to 45 days with a medical certificate.

Maternity leave in the UAE private sector

LeaveLengthPay
Maternity leave60 days45 days full pay, 15 days half pay
Pregnancy or delivery illnessUp to 45 more daysUnpaid, not counted as service
Sick or disabled baby30 days, extendable by 30Full pay, then unpaid
Parental leave (either parent)5 working days within 6 monthsFull pay
Nursing breaksUp to 1 hour a day for 6 monthsPaid

Example: basic salary AED 6,000 and allowances AED 3,000 (gross AED 9,000).

Daily wage = 9,000 ÷ 30 = AED 300.
45 days on full pay = AED 13,500. 15 days on half pay = AED 2,250.
Maternity pay = AED 15,750 for 60 days, against AED 18,000 of normal pay.

Some employers calculate maternity pay on basic salary only. The law says "full pay" and defines pay as including allowances, so the calculator uses gross pay by default and lets you switch. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
