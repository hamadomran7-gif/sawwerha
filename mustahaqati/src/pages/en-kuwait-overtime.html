Work out your overtime pay

Basic plus regular payments (Article 55).

Overtime rules in Kuwait

ItemRule
Normal hours8 a day and 48 a week (Article 64)
Ramadan36 hours a week, not limited to Muslims
Overtime conditionsA written order and a genuine need; capped at 2 hours a day, 3 days a week, 90 days and 180 hours a year (Article 66)
OvertimeNormal pay + 25%; the law has no night premium
Rest dayPay + 50% plus a substitute rest day (Article 67)
Public holidays (13 days)Double pay plus a substitute day (Article 68)
Hourly wageTotal wage ÷ 26 ÷ 8 in common practice

Example: Total wage KWD 6,000, so the hourly wage is 6,000 ÷ 26 ÷ 8 = KWD 28.846. Ten overtime hours: 10 × 28.846 × 1.25 = 360.577; four rest-day hours: 4 × 28.846 × 1.5 = 173.077. Total KWD 533.654 plus a substitute rest day.

The 26-day divisor comes from practice and Article 67, which works out the daily wage from actual working days; some employers divide by 30, so pick the alternative to see the difference.
