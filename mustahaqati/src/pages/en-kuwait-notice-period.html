Notice rules in Kuwait

ItemRule
Notice periodThree months for monthly-paid workers and one month for others, on open-ended contracts (Article 44)
Who must give itBoth sides; it does not vary with length of service
Pay in lieuEqual to the worker's wage for the period not served
Job searchIf the employer gives notice: a full day or 8 hours a week on full pay
Release from workThe employer may release the worker during notice on full pay, still counted as service
ProbationUp to 100 working days, ended without notice (Article 32)

Example: A monthly wage of KWD 8,000 and 3 years of service: three months' notice. If the employer ends the contract immediately, pay in lieu is KWD 24,000; a worker who walks out immediately owes the same.
