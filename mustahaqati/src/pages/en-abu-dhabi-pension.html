Members who met an old-law condition before 1 December 2023 (such as 25 years of service at any age) keep that right. On the age routes (60, women, and mothers with age), the pension is calculated on at least 15 years.

Examples:
An existing member born on 15 May 1980 leaves on 31 May 2026 after 25 years and 3 months with a 40,000 average: at 46 he meets the 25-year route, the percentage is 80% + 0.25 × 2% = 80.5%, and the pension is AED 32,200. Someone born on 1 July 1980 who leaves on 1 December 2026, aged 46 years and 5 months, would not qualify, because the required age is now 46.5; they qualify from 1 January 2027.
A new member who retires in the future at 55 after 30 years with a 30,000 average: 25 × 3.2% + 5 × 2% = 90%, a pension of AED 27,000.
A new member who reaches 60 after 18 years and 4 months with a 15,000 average: 58.67% = 8,800, raised to the minimum of AED 10,200.
A new member who leaves at 40 after 12 years with a 20,000 average: no pension, and a gratuity of 5 × 1.5 + 5 × 2 + 2 × 3 = 23.5 salaries = AED 470,000.

This is an estimate. In the government sector, months before December 2023 inside the six-year window are valued at the pre-amendment salary, and members with more than 25 years before the amendment receive a separate benefit for the excess.
