Calculate your gratuity after resigning

Basic salary only, without allowances.
Deducted from your service, as the law requires.

The rule: resigning does not reduce gratuity

Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021 sets one gratuity for full-time foreign employees who complete a year or more of continuous service, without distinguishing between resignation and termination by the employer:

  • 21 days of basic salary for each of the first five years.
  • 30 days for each year after that.
  • Capped at two years' pay, with part years pro-rated.

What changed from the old law?

Under Federal Law 8 of 1980, an employee resigning from an unlimited contract received a third of the gratuity after one to three years of service and two thirds after three to five years, and the full amount only after five years. Since the current law took effect on 2 February 2022, that reduction is gone: an employee who resigns after one year receives the full gratuity under the same formula.

Notice period before resigning

Article 43 requires written notice and working through the notice period in your contract, between 30 and 90 days. Whoever ends the contract without observing the notice owes the other side the wage for the notice period or the part not served — see the notice pay calculator.

What you receive with the gratuity

  • Your pay up to the last working day.
  • Cash for unused annual leave, on basic salary.
  • Any other dues in your contract.
