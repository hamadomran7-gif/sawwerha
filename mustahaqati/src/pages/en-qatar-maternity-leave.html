Work out your maternity leave dates

As on the medical certificate.
Paid leave requires one full year (12 months).

Maternity leave rules in Qatar's private sector

ItemRuleBasis
Length and pay50 days on full pay covering before and after the birthArticle 96
Service conditionOne full year with the employerArticle 96
After the birthAt least 35 days, so at most 15 days beforeArticle 96
Illness after the birthUp to 60 continuous or separate unpaid days, with a medical certificateArticle 96
NursingOne hour a day for a year starting after the leaveArticle 97
ProtectionNo termination because of marriage or the leave, and no notice during itArticle 98
Paternity leaveNot provided in the Labour Law—

Example: expected date 10 December 2026, total wage QAR 6,000.
Starting on the birth date: leave until 28 January 2027, back on 29 January. Pay 50 × 200 = QAR 10,000.
Earliest start: 25 November 2026 (15 days before), ending 13 January 2027 with exactly 35 days after the birth.
A woman with only 8 months' service gets no maternity pay, but keeps the nursing hour.

If the birth is late and fewer than 30 days of leave remain after it, the shortfall comes from annual leave or is unpaid; some sources give 35 days instead of 30, and we could not read the official Arabic text. Maternity leave does not reduce other leave. "Full pay" most likely means the total wage, since the law defines the wage as basic plus allowances.

The government sector differs: since 7 October 2025 leave is 3 months on gross salary (6 for twins or a child with a disability), with two nursing hours until the child turns two. For other leave use the Qatar leave calculator, and to compare countries the GCC leave comparison.
