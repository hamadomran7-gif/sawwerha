Calculate your pension

The contributory wage shown on your SIO statement. Capped at BD 4,000.

Private-sector pension conditions in Bahrain

CaseConditionEntitlement
Old-age pensionService ends at 60, with at least 180 months of contributions2% × years of contribution × average wage of the last 5 years
Early retirementBefore 60, with at least 240 monthsThe same pension reduced by age (20%, 15% or 10%)
Lump sumReaching 60 with fewer than 180 months15% × average wage × months of contribution
Less than one year—Refund of the employee's own contributions only

Examples:
Retiring at 60 after 30 years on an average of BD 1,200: 1,200 × 30 ÷ 50 = BD 720 a month (60%).
Early retirement at 52 after 25 years on an average of BD 1,000: BD 500 before the reduction, BD 450 after 10%.
Reaching 60 after 10 years (120 months) on an average of BD 800: a lump sum of 0.15 × 800 × 120 = BD 14,400.

What changed in 2022

  • The pension became 2% per year (1/50) instead of 1/60 in the original text, calculated on a five-year average instead of two years.
  • Women's retirement age became 60, like men's, and the contribution period rose to 180 months at 60 and 240 months for early retirement.
  • The automatic annual pension increase stopped; increases are now by ministerial decision.
  • The employer's share rises gradually to 17% in 2028, and the employee's share to 7%.
