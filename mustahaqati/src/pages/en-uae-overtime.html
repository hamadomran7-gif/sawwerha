Calculate your overtime pay

Overtime rules in the UAE Labour Law

Normal working hours are 8 hours a day or 48 hours a week (Article 17). Anything beyond that is overtime, paid as follows:

SituationPay
Extra hour on a normal working dayHourly wage + 25%
Extra hour between 10 pm and 4 am (except shift workers)Hourly wage + 50%
Work on the weekly rest dayA substitute rest day, or the day's wage + 50%
Work on a public holidayA substitute day, or the day's wage + 50%

Example: basic salary AED 6,000 and 20 ordinary overtime hours.

Daily wage = 6,000 ÷ 30 = AED 200. Hourly wage = 200 ÷ 8 = AED 25.
Overtime hour = 25 × 1.25 = AED 31.25. Overtime pay = 20 × 31.25 = AED 625.

Legal limits and exclusions

  • Overtime may not exceed two hours a day, except in cases of necessity.
  • Working hours are reduced by two hours a day during Ramadan.
  • Senior supervisory staff with the employer's powers, ships' crews and preparatory work of up to one hour outside working hours are excluded from overtime pay.
