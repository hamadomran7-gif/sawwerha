Estimate your pension

Periods between the two dates when you were not contributing.
Basic plus housing. Previous system: average of the last 24 months. New system: average of the highest wages over 180 months.

Examples with an average wage of SAR 10,000:
Previous system, 25 years (300 months) at any age: 10,000 × 300 ÷ 480 = SAR 6,250.
Previous system, 35 years at the normal age: SAR 8,750.
New system, 25 years at 65: 2.25% × 10,000 × 25 = SAR 5,625.
New system, early at 58 after 35 years: 7,875 less 15% = SAR 6,693.75.
