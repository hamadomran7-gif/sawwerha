The civil retirement formula

Pension = last monthly basic salary × months of service ÷ 480, that is one fortieth of the salary for each year of service, up to 100% of the last salary at 40 years. There is no reduction for early pensions under this system. The deduction is 9% of basic salary, matched by the government.

The base is the last salary, not the average of the last two years: the original 1393H text that used the average and capped the pension at four fifths was amended, and the 2024 Social Insurance Law did not change it.

Retirement age for government employees

Civil servants retire at 60 Hijri years, about 58 years and two and a half months in Gregorian years, and service can be extended to 65 by a Council of Ministers decision. Convert your date of birth with the Hijri–Gregorian converter.

Those who on 3 July 2024 were both under 50 Hijri years and under 240 months of service have a new age between 58 years 4 months and 65 Gregorian years depending on their age that day, and need 300 to 360 months for a pension before that age. The formula does not change. Work out your new age with the Saudi retirement age calculator. How this interacts with compulsory retirement at 60 Hijri years has not been settled in a published text.

Military retirement

Military pension = last salary × months of service ÷ 420, one thirty-fifth for each year. It is due with 18 years of actual military service, or 20 years of civil and military service including 8 military, or at age 55. The retirement age varies by rank under the officers' and enlisted service laws.

New government employees from 3 July 2024

Anyone who joined government or private employment for the first time from 3 July 2024 with no earlier periods is under the new Social Insurance Law: 2.25% of the average wage for each year, age 65, and early retirement with 360 months from age 55 with a reduction.
