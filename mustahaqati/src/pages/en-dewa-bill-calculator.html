Industrial electricity is 23 fils up to 10,000 kWh, then 38 fils. Add the monthly fuel surcharge (November 2026: 6 fils per kWh and AED 1.10 per m³), meter charges of AED 5 to 35, and 5% VAT.

Dubai Municipality fees on the bill

  • Housing fee: 5% of the annual rent ÷ 12 a month; resident owners pay 5% of the estimated rental value; UAE nationals are exempt.
  • Sewerage charge: 2 fils per gallon of water in 2026, rising to 2.8 fils from January 2027.

Example: a flat using 1,500 kWh and 20 m³ with an annual rent of AED 120,000, in November 2026: electricity 345 + fuel surcharge 90, water 154 + fuel surcharge 22, meters 10, so DEWA's items come to 621 plus VAT 31.05, sewerage 87.99 and housing fee 500. About AED 1,240 in total.

The calculator gives an estimate; the actual bill follows the meter reading, the number of days in the billing cycle and that month's fuel surcharge.
