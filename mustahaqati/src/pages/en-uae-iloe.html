Calculate your ILOE payout

ILOE categories

CategoryBasic salaryPremiumMonthly cap
AUp to AED 16,000AED 5 a monthAED 10,000
BAbove AED 16,000AED 10 a monthAED 20,000

Example: average basic salary AED 12,000 (Category A). 60% = AED 7,200 a month, below the AED 10,000 cap. Up to AED 21,600 over three months, for a premium of AED 60 a year.

The benefit is 60% of your average basic salary over the last six months, paid monthly for up to three months per claim.

Who is exempt from subscribing?

  • Investors and business owners who own and run their companies.
  • Domestic workers.
  • Workers on temporary contracts.
  • Those under 18.
  • Retirees who receive a pension and have taken a new job.

When does the benefit stop?

When you start a new job, leave the country for good, reach the end of the three months, or if the claim is found to be fraudulent. If you claim and then find a job, you can claim again later once you meet the subscription period again.
