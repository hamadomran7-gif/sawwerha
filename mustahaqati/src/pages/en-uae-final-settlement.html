What goes into a UAE final settlement

ItemHow it is calculatedSalary
Gratuity21 days per year for the first five years, then 30 days, after one year of service, capped at two years' payBasic
Unused leaveDaily wage × days of unused leaveBasic
Unpaid daysDaily wage × days worked but not paidFull
Notice payWage for the unserved notice days, paid by whoever did not respect the noticeFull

Example: basic AED 8,000, full salary AED 12,000, 7 years of service, 10 days of unused leave, and the employer ended the contract with 30 days' notice of which only 10 were worked: gratuity 44,000, leave pay 2,666.67 and notice pay for 20 days 8,000, for a total of AED 54,666.67.
