Three things the table shows

  1. Fixed fees are most of the bill at low consumption: at 10 m³ they are SAR 30 of SAR 45 before VAT.
  2. Every cubic metre above 45 costs SAR 9 including sewage before VAT, so cutting use above that level saves the most.
  3. The difference grows with consumption, because the first tiers rose more than the others, while the price above 60 m³ stays the same (SAR 9).
