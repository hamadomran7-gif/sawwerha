This is Table 1 of Cabinet Resolution 23/2012, in force since 1 January 2012, as published in English by the Federal Authority for Government Human Resources. Housing is split into single and married amounts, and the printed totals match the sum of the items for every grade after correcting two cells confirmed by the table's ratios (housing 40% and 60% and cost of living 20% of basic, with caps). Which of two columns is "cost of living" and which is "social allowance" is not settled; it affects neither the total nor the pension.

The undersecretary's and assistant undersecretary's totals include a special allowance granted by the President and furniture, phone and hospitality allowances.

Other allowances

  • Children's allowance: AED 600 per child with no limit, stopping for a son at 24 and for a daughter on marriage.
  • Qualification allowance: AED 1,000 for a master's and 2,000 for a PhD a month if relevant to the job, under the previous regulation and as reported for the 2023 regulation.
  • Technical allowance: under the previous regulation 50% of basic up to 5,000 for engineers and similar, and 30% up to 2,500 for engineering and technical diploma holders; not verified in the 2023 regulation, so the calculator leaves it out.

Annual increment and bonus

Under the Injazati performance system in force from 1 January 2026, annual increments are due to levels 2 to 5 based on the salary scale and the annual rating. The table does not say explicitly that its "annual allowance" column (AED 70 to 250) is added to basic pay each year, so we offer it as an optional estimate. The annual bonus is a one-off payment: up to twice the total salary for rating 5, 1.5 for rating 4 and one salary for rating 3, capped at AED 100,000 a year.

Pension

11% is deducted for those first registered from 31 October 2023 and 5% for those registered earlier, with the employer paying 15%. The pensionable salary in government is basic pay, housing, cost of living, social allowance and children's allowance, capped at AED 100,000; transport and the President's grant are excluded.
