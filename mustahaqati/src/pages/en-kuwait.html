Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service indemnity15 days' wages for each of the first 5 years, then a month's wage per year, capped at 18 months' wages, on the last full wage.Article 51
Indemnity on resignationNothing before 3 years, half up to 5, two thirds up to 10, and the full amount after.Article 53
Annual leave30 working days, excluding weekly rest days, holidays and sick days, after 6 months of service.Article 70 (2017)
Sick leave15 days on full pay, then 10 at three-quarters, 10 at half, 10 at a quarter, then 30 unpaid.Article 69
Pension (Kuwaitis, Arabic)65% of the pension salary for 15 years plus 2% a year up to 95%, age 55 for men and 50 for women, with early retirement reductions and a minimum from KWD 1,010.Social Insurance Law 61/1976
