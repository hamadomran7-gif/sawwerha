Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service indemnity15 days' wages for each of the first 5 years, then a month's wage per year, capped at 18 months' wages, on the last full wage.Article 51
Indemnity on resignationNothing before 3 years, half up to 5, two thirds up to 10, and the full amount after.Article 53
Annual leave30 working days, excluding weekly rest days, holidays and sick days, after 6 months of service.Article 70 (2017)
Sick leave15 days on full pay, then 10 at three-quarters, 10 at half, 10 at a quarter, then 30 unpaid.Article 69
Maternity leave70 days on full pay if the birth falls within them, up to 4 unpaid months with the employer's approval, and two nursing hours a day.Articles 24 and 25
Pension (Kuwaitis)65% of the pension salary for 15 years plus 2% a year up to 95%, age 55 for men and 50 for women, with early retirement reductions and a minimum from KWD 1,010.Social Insurance Law 61/1976
Net salary after PIFSS10.5% for Kuwaitis in the private sector and 10% in government up to 1,500, the supplementary band to 2,750, 11.5% from the employer, and nothing for expats.Social Insurance Law 61/1976
National labour supportFor Kuwaitis in the private sector: KWD 456–790 single and 534–898 married by qualification, 50 per child up to 7, and a 50 deduction if the salary exceeds 1,250.Law 19/2000 and Decision 391/2001
OvertimePay + 25%; rest day + 50% with a substitute day; holidays double pay with a substitute day; capped at 2 hours a day and 180 a year.Articles 64–68
Notice periodThree months for monthly pay and one month otherwise, for both sides; pay in lieu equals the wage.Article 44
Residency finesExpired residence KD 2 a day in the first month then 4, up to 1,200; domestic workers 2 up to 600; visit visas 10 up to 2,000.Decree-Law 114/2024
Public holidays13 paid days; work on them earns double pay plus a substitute day.Article 68
