What a final settlement in Oman includes

ItemHow it is calculatedArticle
End-of-service gratuityOne month's basic per year since 31/7/2023, and before that 15 days per year for the first 3 years then a month61 (old law 39)
Leave payFull wage ÷ 30 × unused days at the end of service78–81
Notice30 days for monthly-paid workers, on the last full wage36–39
Unpaid salaryFull wage ÷ 30 × unpaid days—

Example: Basic 600 and allowances 200, 6 years and 10 months, and 10 leave days: gratuity 3,200.960 and leave pay 266.667, a total of OMR 3,467.627.

Notice pay covers the unserved days: the employer pays it when ending the contract without full notice, and it is deducted from a worker who leaves without it. For detail use the end-of-service calculator and the leave calculator.
