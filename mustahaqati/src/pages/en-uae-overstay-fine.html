Calculate the fine

Grace period after a residence visa expires or is cancelled

CategoryGrace period
Golden, Green and Blue residence holders and their families, widows and divorcees of residents, students after finishing their studies, and relatives of UAE nationals with foreign passports180 days
Skilled workers at levels 1 to 3, and property owners90 days
Residence permits issued with a guarantor or host (employees, families and domestic workers according to press reports)60 days
All other categories (including retirees, remote workers, investors and partners)30 days
Visit or tourist visaNone

After the grace period, a fine of AED 50 a day applies "starting from the day following the end of the grace period", the same for residence and visit visas across all emirates since fines were unified. We found no published cap. GDRFA Dubai gives retirees 60 days, the only known difference between emirates in grace periods.

Other fines and fees

ItemAmount
Late Emirates ID renewalAED 20 a day up to AED 1,000, plus the renewal fee of AED 100 a year + 100
Newborn residence after 120 days from birthAED 50 a day (the government portal says 100); the baby cannot leave without residence
Departure permit for violatorsAED 200 + 100 smart services, leaving within 7 days
Status change inside the country instead of leavingAED 500
Work permit not renewed by the employer within 60 daysAED 200 a month up to 2,000 (payable by the company)

Examples:
Work residence cancelled on 1 August 2026, leaving on 15 October: last fine-free day 30 September, then 15 days × 50 = AED 750.
The same case for a retiree (30 days): last day 31 August, then 45 days × 50 = AED 2,250.
Visit visa expired on 10 September, leaving on 20 September: 10 days × 50 = AED 500.

Results are estimates and may differ by one day depending on how days are counted. A long overstay or unpaid fines can lead to referral and deportation, and a deported person can only return with permission. You can apply for a full or partial waiver of the fine. If you are leaving your job, calculate your final settlement before the visa is cancelled.
