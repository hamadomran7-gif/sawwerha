Hazardous or arduous work, military and security service and persons with disabilities have lower ages under special conditions in Article 75. Old age is 60 and rises by one year every seven years up to 65.

How the pension is calculated

  • Service since 2024: 2% × the average future value of your wages × the years of that service. Each year's wage is multiplied by wage growth for every following year up to entitlement.
  • Earlier service: 2% × your final salary under the previous system, multiplied by wage growth from 2024 to entitlement, × the years of earlier service.
  • Reduction: applies to both parts together, and the pension after it cannot fall below the cash guarantee, if any.

Example: a man born on 1 Jan 1970 with 20 years of service before 2024 on a final salary of OMR 1,200, a steady wage of OMR 1,300 since 2024, retiring on 1 Jan 2030 at 60.

Revalued final salary: 1,200 × 1.03⁶ = 1,432.86, so the first part is 2% × 20 × 1,432.86 = 573.15.
The average future value of his wages since 2024 is about 1,443.53, so the second part is 2% × 6 × 1,443.53 = 173.22.
Estimated pension: OMR 746.37 a month, or the cash guarantee if higher.
