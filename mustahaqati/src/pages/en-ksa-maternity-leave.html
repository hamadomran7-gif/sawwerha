Plan your maternity leave

As stated in the medical certificate.
No more than 4 weeks before the expected date.

Maternity leave after the Article 151 amendment

Since 19 February 2025, maternity leave in the private sector has been 12 weeks on full pay, with no minimum service. The worker may arrange it as she wishes, on two conditions: it may not start more than 4 weeks before the expected date of birth, and 6 weeks of it must fall after the birth.

Leave around childbirth in Saudi Arabia

LeaveLengthPay
Maternity leave12 weeks, up to 4 before the birth and at least 6 afterFull pay
Optional extension1 monthUnpaid
Sick or disabled baby1 month, extendable by 1 monthFull pay, then unpaid
Paternity leave3 days within 7 days of the birthFull pay

Example: expected date 1 June 2026, leave from 4 May 2026 (four weeks before). The 84 days end on 26 July 2026, and you are back at work on 27 July — provided the baby arrives on time.

Pay continues in full throughout the leave: on an actual wage of SAR 9,000 that is 84 days × 300 = SAR 25,200, with no deduction. With the optional unpaid month, the return date in the example moves to 27 August 2026 and that month is unpaid.

The ministry's awareness guide says the nursing hour lasts 24 months, while the article itself sets no period. Dates for every type of leave are in the Saudi special leave calculator. A worker who resigns within three months of giving birth keeps her full end-of-service award (Article 87); see the end-of-service calculator.
