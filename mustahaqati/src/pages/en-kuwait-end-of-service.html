Calculate your indemnity

Basic salary plus regular allowances.

How the indemnity is calculated (Article 51/b, monthly pay)

  • 15 days' wage for each of the first five years.
  • A month's wage for each year after that.
  • Cap: a year and a half's wage (18 months).
  • Part years count pro rata, with no minimum service when the employer ends the contract. The daily wage is the monthly wage ÷ 26.

Workers paid daily, hourly or by piece get 10 days' wage for each of the first 5 years, then 15 days, capped at a year's wage (Article 51/a).

Kuwait indemnity rules on resignation

ServiceOn resignation (Article 53)
Less than 3 yearsNothing
3 to under 5 yearsHalf
5 to under 10 yearsTwo thirds
10 years or moreFull

Example: last wage KWD 800 and 7 years of service, ended by the employer. Daily wage 800 ÷ 26 = 30.769. First five years: 5 × 15 days = KWD 2,307.69. Two more years: 2 × 800 = KWD 1,600. Indemnity: KWD 3,907.69. On resignation: two thirds = KWD 2,605.13.

Indemnity for Kuwaitis

The same formula applies to Kuwaitis and non-Kuwaitis. Since Article 51 was amended by Law 17 of 2018, the employer may not deduct its social insurance contributions from a Kuwaiti worker's indemnity.
