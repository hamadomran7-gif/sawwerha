The 9-month guarantee under Decision 61/2026

Labour Minister Decision 61/2026, regulating licences to recruit workers from abroad on behalf of others, was published in the Official Gazette on 25 October 2026 and applies from the day after publication. It repeals Decision 8/2005 and gives offices 3 months to comply:

  • 3-month preliminary test period: the full amount paid is refunded minus government fees if the worker is unfit, breaches the contract, refuses to work or runs away, or if the employer ends the contract.
  • 6 additional months: 15% of the total amount is deducted for each month, for refusal, running away or chronic illness, and the office bears the government fees.
  • Loss of the right: if the employer or the family assaults the worker, breaches the contract, or does not pay the wage.
  • Office financial guarantee: QAR 750,000 instead of 250,000.

How much is a housemaid's salary in Qatar?

The minimum wage is QAR 1,000 basic, plus QAR 500 for housing and QAR 300 for food a month if the employer does not provide them. So the lowest monthly cost for a live-in worker is QAR 1,000, and QAR 1,800 for a worker given neither housing nor food.

Examples:
Filipina at the maximum with a QAR 1,000 wage, housing and food provided: 15,000 + 1,000 × 12 = QAR 27,000 in the first year, QAR 7,500 of it at contract.
Running away in month 5 on a QAR 15,000 cost: 30% is deducted, so QAR 10,500 is refunded.
Refusing to work in month 2 with QAR 1,200 of government fees: QAR 13,800 is refunded.
