Examples:
A government employee insured before 2023 reaching retirement age after 27 years on a last contribution salary of QAR 24,000: the period is settled on 30 years (Article 82), so the pension is QAR 24,000 (100%). After 17 years it would be settled on 27 years, giving 21,600.
For someone who joined after 2023, Article 82 does not apply: 27 years on 24,000 gives 24,000 × 27 ÷ 30 = QAR 21,600, and 25 years on 16,000 gives 13,333, raised to the government minimum of QAR 15,000.
Resigning at 52 after 27 years on 24,000: 21,600 reduced by (60 − 52) × 2.5% = 20%, so QAR 17,280, not raised to the minimum.
Private sector after 30 years on a 3-year average of 20,000 with 14,000 in the first year: the cap is 130% × 14,000 = 18,200, so QAR 18,200.
