The tables are generated with the same rules as the UAE pension calculator and assume you qualify for a pension (age 60 with 15 years, or the resignation conditions).

Old law (registered before 31 October 2023)

{{table:uae-pension-grid lang=en}}

Pension = average contribution salary of the last 5 years × (60% for 15 years + 2% for each extra year), up to 100% at 35 years, with the contribution salary capped at AED 50,000.

New law (first registered from 31 October 2023)

{{table:uae-pension-grid system=new lang=en}}

Pension = average contribution salary of the last 6 years × (2.67% a year up to 30, then 4%), up to 100%, with the contribution salary capped at AED 70,000. Asterisk: raised to the AED 10,000 minimum.

Abu Dhabi Pension Fund (Abu Dhabi nationals)

{{table:uae-pension-grid system=ad lang=en}}

Pension = average of the last 6 years × (3.2% a year up to 25, then 2%), up to 100% at 35 years, with the contribution salary capped at AED 150,000 for existing members. Asterisk: raised to the AED 10,200 minimum. For eligibility and the early-retirement routes, use the Abu Dhabi pension calculator.

What the table leaves out

  • Members who do not qualify for a pension get an end-of-service gratuity instead: 1.5 months' salary for each of the first five years, 2 months for each of the next five and 3 months after that.
  • Sharjah government employees are under a separate system.
  • Periodic pension increases decided by the authorities are not included.
