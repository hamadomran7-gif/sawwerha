Calculate your overtime pay

Housing, transport and others — part of the actual wage.

The overtime formula

Overtime hourly rate = actual hourly wage + 50% of the basic hourly wage, where the actual wage is basic salary plus fixed allowances, and each hourly wage is the monthly figure ÷ 30 ÷ 8.

Example (the ministry's own): basic SAR 4,000 and allowances SAR 2,000 (actual wage SAR 6,000).

Actual hourly wage = 6,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = SAR 25. Basic hourly wage = 4,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = SAR 16.67, and half of it is SAR 8.33.
Overtime rate = SAR 33.33 an hour; 3 hours = SAR 100.

A common mistake is to pay 1.5 × the basic hourly wage. On a basic salary of SAR 10,000 with SAR 4,000 of allowances and 20 overtime hours, the correct formula gives 58.33 + 20.83 = SAR 79.17 an hour, or SAR 1,583.33, while 1.5 × basic gives only SAR 1,250.

What counts as overtime

  • Hours beyond 8 a day, or beyond 48 a week where the weekly standard is used.
  • All hours worked on rest days and public holidays.
  • In Ramadan: hours beyond 6 a day or 36 a week for Muslim workers.

Time off in lieu

Since 2025, with your consent, the employer may give paid compensatory leave instead of pay: at least 1.5 hours off for each overtime hour, used within 60 days, up to 30 days a year.
