How long until payday?

Federal government salaries

{{table:uae-pay-dates item=federal from=2026-11 count=14 lang=en}}

The 30th of each month under a Ministry of Finance circular (March 2019), moved earlier if it falls on a holiday. Since the government weekend has been Saturday and Sunday since 2022, we move it to the Friday, and in February to the last working day; both are our inference. We found no official restatement of the day after 2023, and payment may be brought forward before Eid by directive. The Abu Dhabi and Dubai governments have not published a usual payday.

GPSSA pensions

{{table:uae-pay-dates item=gpssa from=2026-11 count=14 lang=en}}

2026 dates are from GPSSA's official schedule, based on the 27th and adjusted for holidays. 2027 dates are estimates on the same basis until the schedule is published in January; they may differ by a day or two or be brought forward before Eid.

Abu Dhabi Pension Fund

The 26th of each month according to the fund, with early payments announced before Eid (16 March, 20 April and 22 May 2026). It has not said what happens when the 26th falls on a Saturday or Sunday, as on 26 December 2026. Estimate your pension with the Abu Dhabi pension calculator.

Private-sector pay: due on the 1st, late from the 2nd

Since 1 June 2026 wages are due on the 1st of each calendar month for the previous month under Ministerial Resolution 0340/2026 on the Wages Protection System, replacing the contract date or monthly payment with a 15-day grace period (Resolution 598/2022). A company counts as compliant if it transfers 85% of the wages due on time. Escalation, in days from the due date:

  • Day 2: electronic monitoring and notice.
  • Day 5: new work permits stopped.
  • Day 11: for repeat cases within 6 months, a fine and a lower company category.
  • Day 16: disputes registered automatically.
  • Day 21: an enforcement order or collective dispute, precautionary attachment and travel bans, and referral to prosecutors for repeat cases.

The coverage we found does not say what happens when the due date is a holiday, such as December 2026 pay due on Friday 1 January 2027. Work out your take-home pay with the UAE net salary calculator.
