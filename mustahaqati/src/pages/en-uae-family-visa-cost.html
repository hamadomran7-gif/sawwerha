Example: a wife and two children, two-year residence, family outside the UAE.
In Dubai: entry 630, residence 720, IDs 900 and the wife's medical 320 = AED 2,570 in government fees, or 4,370 with a year of insurance (600 per person); then about 2,770 a year with insurance.
In Abu Dhabi: entry 900, residence 1,200, IDs 900 and medical 250 = AED 3,250, then about 1,175 a year.

Sponsorship requires a salary of at least AED 4,000, or 3,000 with employer housing, and an attested tenancy contract. A dependent's residence cannot outlast the sponsor's. The figures above are published government fees, unchanged in 2025–2026 as far as we found; service centres add AED 100–300 per application, and newspapers put the real first-year cost in Dubai at about AED 7,000 for a family of three.
