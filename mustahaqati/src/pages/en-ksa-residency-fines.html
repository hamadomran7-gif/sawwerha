Amounts are in Saudi riyals. The iqama fine is per offence and per iqama, not per day, and is paid with the renewal fees. The employer bears the worker's residence fees, their renewal and late fines (Article 40 of the Labour Law), while family members' iqamas are paid by the head of the family.

Example: a worker with his wife and two children renews all four iqamas late for the first time: 4 × 500 = SAR 2,000 on top of the renewal fees, of which 500 falls on the employer for the worker and 1,500 on the worker for his family. The second time it would be 4,000.
