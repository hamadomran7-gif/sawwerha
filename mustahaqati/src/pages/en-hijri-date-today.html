About the Umm al-Qura calendar

The Umm al-Qura calendar is Saudi Arabia's official Hijri calendar. It is calculated astronomically, so month starts are known in advance, and government transactions, residence permits and contracts rely on it. The start of Ramadan, Shawwal and Dhu al-Hijjah is announced by moon sighting, so it can differ from the calendar by a day, and between countries. The calculator uses the "islamic-umalqura" calendar built into your browser, which covers the years 1300 to 1600 AH.

To convert any date between the two calendars use the Hijri–Gregorian date converter; for your age in Hijri years, the Hijri age calculator; and for the gap between two dates, the days between dates calculator.
