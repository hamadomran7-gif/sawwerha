When VAT started in April 2021, M95 went from 216 to 227 baisa, exactly 5% more. We last checked the prices on 3 November 2026 and will update this page if a change is announced.

How to work out your fuel cost

Full tank = tank size × price a litre. Monthly cost = (distance a month × litres per 100 km ÷ 100) × price a litre. A car that covers 2,000 km a month at 8 litres per 100 km needs 160 litres, which is OMR 36.640 with M91, or OMR 439.680 a year.
