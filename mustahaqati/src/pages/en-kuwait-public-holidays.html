Pay for working on a holiday

If the work requires an employee to work on one of these days, they are entitled to double pay for the day plus a substitute day (Article 68). We calculate the day's wage as total pay ÷ 26 and the amount owed as twice that on top of salary, as published legal columns read it. Another reading treats "double" as including the holiday pay already in salary, so the extra would be one day's wage.

Example: total salary KD 800 and one working day on Eid: 800 ÷ 26 = 30.77, so you are owed KD 61.54 plus a day off. On the minimum reading it is 30.77, and dividing salary by 30 gives 53.33.

Holidays that fall on the rest day

The law gives the private sector no day in lieu when a holiday falls on the weekly rest day. The Cabinet sets linking rest days for the government sector for each occasion, and they do not apply to the private sector by law.

2027 holidays

No private-sector holidays remain in 2026. In 2027 Isra and Mi'raj comes twice (around 5 January and 25 December), and National Day and Liberation Day fall on Thursday and Friday 25–26 February.

Hijri dates are expected by the Umm al-Qura calendar and may move by a day; the Cabinet announces holidays before each occasion.
