Calculate the visa fee

A month is 30 days; part of a month counts as a full month.
Each family member needs their own visa and fee.

Exit and re-entry visa fees by length

LengthSingle (SAR)Multiple (SAR)
Up to 2 months (60 days)200500
3 months (90 days)300500
4 months400700
6 months6001,100
12 months1,2002,300
Extra month from inside100200
Extra month from abroad200400

Fees are per person. The visa cannot run beyond the iqama's validity, and it is issued only while the traveller is in Saudi Arabia with a valid iqama and a passport valid for at least 90 days.

Example: a family of five (worker, wife and 3 children) on a single visa for 3 months: 5 × 300 = SAR 1,500, of which 300 is paid by the employer and 1,200 by the head of family. Extending everyone by one month from abroad: 5 × 200 = SAR 1,000.
