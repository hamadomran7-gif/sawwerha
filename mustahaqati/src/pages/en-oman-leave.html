Annual leave in Oman

  • At least 30 days on gross pay a year; it does not increase with seniority.
  • It cannot be taken in the first 6 months of employment.
  • Up to 30 days may be carried over, and 30 days must be taken at least once every two years.
  • Unused days are paid at gross pay at the end of service, and at basic pay with written consent during service.

Example: gross pay OMR 1,000 (basic 600) and 20 unused days.
At the end of service: 1,000 ÷ 30 × 20 = OMR 666.67.
During service with written consent: 600 ÷ 30 × 20 = OMR 400.
