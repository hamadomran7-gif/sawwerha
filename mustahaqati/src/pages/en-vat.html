The two formulas

  • Adding VAT: inclusive amount = amount × (1 + rate).
  • Removing VAT: amount before VAT = inclusive amount ÷ (1 + rate); the VAT is the difference.

Example: an invoice of SAR 2,300 including VAT in Saudi Arabia. Before VAT = 2,300 ÷ 1.15 = SAR 2,000, and the VAT is SAR 300.
