Work out your net salary

Private: everything paid in cash or in kind. Government: basic, social and children's allowances.

Contribution rates for Kuwaitis

BranchEmployeeEmployerBand
Basic insurance5%10%up to 1,500
Supplementary insurance5%10%1,500–2,750

Financial reward2.5%—up to 1,500

Examples (private sector):
Salary 1,400: 10.5% = 147 is deducted and net pay is KWD 1,253; the employer pays 161.
Salary 2,000: 75 + 25 + 50 + 37.5 + 10 = 197.5 is deducted and net pay is 1,802.5; the employer pays 230.
Salary 14,000: insurable pay is capped at 2,750, so 257.5 is deducted and net pay is 13,742.5.

Insurable pay in the private sector is everything paid in cash or in kind; in government it is basic plus social and children's allowances. None of the rates or caps changed in 2024–2026. Secondary sources say national labour support may count as insurable pay, but we found no official text. The treasury pays further shares that do not affect your net pay.
