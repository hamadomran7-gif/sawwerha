Increments and promotion

  • Annual increment: moves the officer to the next step in his rank, in January in practice since 2018, with none after the last step.
  • Captain and Major increments: a Captain gets 495 to step 7 and then 265, and a Major 265, a shape inherited from the annex itself (430 then 230, and 230).
  • Promotion: onto the first step of the new rank, or the first step above current pay if that is higher.
  • Lieutenant General and General: have no table and are treated like the Excellent rank and the minister's rank.

Transport and other allowances

Transport under Article 59 as amended by Royal Decree M/32 (5/6/1434H): 700 for Second Lieutenants, First Lieutenants and Captains, 900 for Majors, Lieutenant Colonels and Colonels, 1,200 for Brigadier Generals, and civil rank 15 treatment for Major Generals (a government car or SAR 2,000 until one is provided). The SAR 500 monthly allowance for civilian and military staff is added from January 2027 and is not part of basic pay. Duty, hazard, regional and other allowances vary by unit and job and are not included.

Deductions

9% of basic is deducted for military retirement, and the state pays 13%. Estimate your pension with the civil and military retirement calculator, and see enlisted pay in the military salary scale.

Examples:
Second Lieutenant, step 1: 7,590 + 700 = 8,290, deduction 683.10, net SAR 7,606.90, plus 500 from January 2027.
Captain, step 9: 13,570 + 2 × 265 = 14,100, 14,800 with transport, net SAR 13,531.
A Captain at step 15 (15,690) promoted to Major: the first step above his pay is step 10 (15,900), a raise of only 210.
