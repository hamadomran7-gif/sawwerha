Calculate the maximum increase

From the Dubai Smart Rental Index on the DLD website or the Dubai REST app.
To see the last date for notice of an increase.

Decree 43 of 2013 increase bands

Your rent is below the index average byMaximum increase on renewal
Up to 10% (or above the average)No increase
11% to 20%5%
21% to 30%10%
31% to 40%15%
More than 40%20%

Example: your rent is AED 80,000 and the index average for a similar unit is AED 100,000. Your rent is 20% below, so the maximum increase is 5% = AED 4,000, and the new rent may not exceed AED 84,000.

Rules to know

  • When: only on renewal, never during the lease.
  • Notice: at least 90 days before the lease expires.
  • Index: since January 2025 the Dubai Smart Rental Index sets the average; the Decree 43 percentages are unchanged.
  • Scope: all landlords in Dubai, including special development zones and free zones.
