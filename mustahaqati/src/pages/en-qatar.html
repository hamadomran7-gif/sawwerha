Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service gratuity21 days of the last basic salary for each year after one year of service, with no cap, and not reduced on resignation.Article 54
Final settlementGratuity of 21 days of basic per year, leave pay, one or two months' notice, and unpaid salary.Law 14/2004 Articles 49, 54 and 79
Annual leave3 weeks for service under 5 years and 4 weeks after, paid on basic salary ÷ 30.Articles 79 and 81
Sick leaveAfter 3 months of service: two weeks on full pay, then 4 weeks on half pay, then unpaid.Article 82
Maternity leave50 days on full pay after a year of service, 35 of them after the birth; a nursing hour a day for a year after the leave; no paternity leave.Articles 96–98
Pension (Qataris)1/30 of the pension salary per year up to 100%, 25 years including 20 actual, a 2.5% a year reduction on resignation, and a QAR 15,000 government minimum.Law 1/2022, Arts 24, 29, 34, 36
Net salary after pension7% for Qataris and 14% from the employer on basic, social allowance and housing up to 6,000, capped at 100,000, and nothing for expats.Law 1/2022
Qatari government salary scale14 grades from 12 to Excellent, social allowance 6,400 or 4,000, housing up to 6,000, and the 1 January increment at 150% to 0% by performance.Law 15/2016, Law 25/2025 and Decision 34/2025
Family residence costVisa 200, residence permit 400 a year and ID 100, and the QAR 10,000 (or 6,000 with family housing) salary rule.Interior Ministry (December 2023)
OvertimeBasic + 25% by day and + 50% from 9 pm to 3 am; rest day a substitute day or + 150%; 10-hour daily limit.Articles 73–78
Notice periodOne month up to two years of service, two months after, for both sides, with pay in lieu on basic.Article 49 (2020)
Payment of duesBefore the end of the day after the contract ends, or within 7 days if the worker leaves without observing notice.Article 67
Overstay fines14 days to leave after a residence permit is cancelled, then QR 10 a day; visit visas QR 200 a day.Law 21/2015 and MOI statement 2026
Housemaid recruitment costMaximum by nationality (Philippines 15,000, Ethiopia 9,000) covering all fees except quarantine, and a 9-month guarantee: full refund for 3 months, then 15% deducted per month.MOCI Decision 1/2022 and MoL Decision 61/2026
Public holidays3 working days per Eid, National Day on 18 December and 3 days set by the employer; work on them at basic + 25%.Articles 74 and 78
School holidays 2026–2027Mid-year 20 Dec – 3 Jan, long weekend 31 Mar – 1 Apr, staff leave from 27 Jun.Annual school calendar
