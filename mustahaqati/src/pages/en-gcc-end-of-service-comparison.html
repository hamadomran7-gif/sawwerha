A fixed wage of 10,000 in each local currency, whole years, rounded. Kuwait divides the wage by 26, and Oman assumes all service is after 31 July 2023. The figures compare rules, not purchasing power, since the currencies differ.

Three observations

  1. The wage base changes everything: an employee whose basic is half the salary gets half as much in the UAE or Qatar as the same formula gives in Saudi Arabia or Kuwait.
  2. Early resignation is costly only in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait: nothing before 3 years in Kuwait, and before two years in Saudi Arabia.
  3. Systems are moving to savings funds: Bahrain since March 2024, Oman by July 2027 at the latest, the UAE has an optional alternative savings scheme, and Qatar is studying a similar system.
