How the resignation award is calculated: two steps

  1. Full award (Article 84): half a month's wage for each of the first five years and a full month's wage for each year after that, on the last actual wage, with part years pro rata.
  2. Resignation fraction (Article 85): you receive part of the full award depending on your service:
Service at resignationWhat you receive
Under 2 yearsNothing
2 to 5 yearsOne third of the award
Over 5 and under 10 yearsTwo thirds of the award
10 years or moreThe full award

Example (the calculator above): actual wage SAR 10,000, 4 years 4 months of service, resignation.

Full award = 4.333 × 5,000 = SAR 21,667.
Service between two and five years → one third: SAR 7,222.

When you get the full award despite resigning

  • Leaving because of force majeure beyond your control (Article 87).
  • A female employee ending her contract within six months of marriage or three months of childbirth (Article 87).
  • Leaving under one of the Article 81 cases, which keep all your rights: the seven cases.
