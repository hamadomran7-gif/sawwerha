Calculate your gratuity after dismissal

Basic salary only, without allowances.
Deducted from your service, as the law requires.

The 10 cases in Article 44

The employer may dismiss a worker without notice, after a written investigation and with a reasoned written decision, in these cases:

CaseExtra condition
1. Impersonating someone else, or submitting forged certificates or documentsProven
2. A mistake causing serious material loss, or deliberate damage to the employer's property, with admissionMinistry notified within 7 working days of learning of it
3. Breaking the internal rules on workplace and worker safetyRules written, posted visibly and communicated to the worker
4. Failing to perform basic duties and continuing to do soWritten investigation and two warnings of dismissal if repeated
5. Disclosing the business's industrial or intellectual property secretsCausing loss, a lost opportunity or personal gain
6. Being visibly drunk or under the influence of drugs at work, or an indecent act at the workplace—
7. Assaulting the employer, manager, superiors or colleagues during work—
8. Absence without a valid reason for more than 20 separate days in a year or more than 7 consecutive days—
9. Exploiting the job position unlawfully for personal gain—
10. Joining another business without following the rules and procedures—

Your gratuity is preserved

Under the old law, dismissal for these reasons cancelled the gratuity. Decree-Law 33 of 2021 makes dismissal a disciplinary penalty with preservation of the worker's right to end-of-service gratuity. Only the notice and notice pay are lost.

Example (the calculator above): basic salary AED 7,000 and 5 years 5 months of service, dismissed under Article 44.

Daily wage = 7,000 ÷ 30 = AED 233.33.
First 5 years × 21 days = 105 days = AED 24,500; the next five months × 30 days pro rata = 12.5 days = AED 2,917.
Gratuity ≈ AED 27,417 — the same as if the contract ended for any other reason, without notice pay.

If the employer did not follow the procedure

Dismissal without a written investigation, without a written reasoned decision, or for a reason outside the ten cases becomes a termination without notice, so the worker is owed notice pay on top of the gratuity.
