How to compare the real cost

The maximum price is only half the picture. Saudi Arabia adds visa and iqama fees, and the average actually paid there is below the cap for African nationalities (Ethiopia about SAR 3,972 in July 2026). Qatar includes all fees in the price, but its minimum wage is higher. Kuwait is cheaper on price and residence for Kuwaiti families, and a Filipina's wage there is KWD 120 to 150. Work out each country with your own figures in its calculator, then compare the first-year cost.

Example: a Filipina at the maximum price, first year:
Saudi Arabia: 16,905 + 2,000 + 172.50 + 600 + salary 1,500 × 12 = SAR 37,677.50.
Qatar: 15,000 + 1,000 × 12 = QAR 27,000 with housing and food provided.
Kuwait: 750 + residence 10 + 120 × 12 = KWD 2,200 for a Kuwaiti family.

After recruitment, work out the worker's end-of-contract entitlements in Saudi Arabia or the UAE, and the Saudi transfer fee.
