Estimate your pension

Decides the law: before or from 31 October 2023.
New law: average of the last 6 years. Old law (private sector): average of the last 5 years.

The two laws side by side

ItemLaw 7/1999Decree-Law 57/2023
Applies toRegistered before 31 October 2023First registered from 31 October 2023
Pension salaryAverage of the last 5 years (private sector)Average of the last 6 years
Rate60% for 15 years + 2% a year2.67% a year up to 30, then 4%
Maximum100% at 35 years100% at 35 years
By age60 with 15 years60 with 15 years
On resignation20 years and the specified age30 years and age 55
Minimum pensionNot set for the private sector in the sources we reviewedAED 10,000
Employee contribution5%11%

Examples:
New law, 30 years at 55, average AED 17,500: 80.1% × 17,500 = AED 14,017.50.
New law, 15 years at 60, average AED 18,000: 40.05% = 7,209, raised to AED 10,000.
Old law, 20 years, average AED 20,000 (joined before 2007 and aged 50): 70% = AED 14,000.

This is an estimate; GPSSA sets the official pension. Abu Dhabi nationals in the Abu Dhabi Pension Fund should use the Abu Dhabi pension calculator. Expats in the private sector should use the gratuity calculator. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
