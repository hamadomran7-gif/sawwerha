Calculate the cost-of-living allowance

Salary or pension and property income. The wife's income is not counted, as reported from the ministry.

Allowance amounts from January 2026

{{table:bh-col-brackets lang=en}}

On 31 December 2025 the Crown Prince and Prime Minister ordered a BHD 20 increase in each bracket of financial support for low-income families from January 2026. The bracket follows the head of household's income only, and the amount does not change with family size. The order says "less than 300" for the first bracket while the ministry's reply says "300 or less", so we treat exactly 300 as the first bracket.

Who is eligible

  • Nationality and residence: Bahraini and permanently resident, with exceptions for work or treatment abroad with documents.
  • Income: the head of household earns BHD 1,000 a month or less.
  • Groups: married couples with or without children, widowers with children, divorced parents with custody, elderly people in certain social situations, and social security recipients automatically without applying.
  • Commercial register: one register with one branch is accepted, with up to 10 workers and total wages up to BHD 1,000.

Single people who are not elderly are not in the official list of groups, so they are most likely not eligible.

Payment date and applying

The allowance is paid on the 15th of each month, the single day the Ministry of Labour and Social Development set in 2020 for social security, the cost-of-living allowance, the disability allowance and the housing allowance; we found nothing changing it. Apply in person at a social centre with your smart card and a bank statement showing your IBAN, plus divorce and custody papers, commercial register documents or a salary certificate if needed; it takes one working day. Beneficiaries must report any change in income or circumstances.

Other support programmes

  • Social security: a minimum of BHD 77 for one person, 132 for two and 28 for each extra member since January 2022, with a BHD 10 to 20 discount on the electricity and water bill.
  • Disability allowance: BHD 100, or 200 for severe cases such as total paralysis, autism and severe intellectual disability, not linked to salary.
  • Retirees' living allowance: BHD 190 for pensions under 700 and 165 under 1,500 since January 2023; the 2025–2026 budget approved an annual increase whose amount we could not find. It is paid with the pension.

Examples:
A couple with two children and a husband's salary of 280: first bracket, BHD 130 a month and 1,560 a year.
A family of seven with a head of household earning 650 and a working wife: second bracket, BHD 97, since the wife's income is not counted.
A divorced parent with custody earning 950: BHD 75. A couple on 1,050: no allowance.
