This table circulates on specialist sites and we found no official published copy, so our confidence is medium. But it is arithmetically consistent in every cell (a fixed difference between steps), and the private's first step of 3,220 equals exactly the 2010 scale (2,800) after the 15% cost-of-living allowance was merged into basic pay in 1432H. We found no news of any change to military basic pay after 2011; later changes were to allowances. The older table in which a private starts at SAR 1,000 predates the 2010 raise and is no longer used.

Enlisted allowances

  • Transport: SAR 700 for master sergeants and staff sergeants, and SAR 500 for sergeants, lance sergeants, corporals, privates first class and privates (2012 amendment to the rights and benefits regulation).
  • Danger and infection: raised for enlisted personnel to SAR 600 and 750 in 2013, as reported by Okaz, for work with explosives, aircrews, infectious diseases, radiology and laboratories.
  • SAR 500 allowance: from 1 January 2027 for officers, enlisted personnel and retirees, not added to basic pay.
  • Housing: we found no basis for a general housing allowance for the military; the percentages in circulation (25% or 30%) have no source.

Technical, security, aviation, paratrooper and special forces allowances vary by unit and job, and we found no reliable recent source for their amounts, so the calculator does not include them.

Increments and promotion

The annual increment moves a soldier to the next step. Article 15 of the Enlisted Service Law says 1 Muharram, but since 2018 it has been paid with the January salary, provided a year has passed in the rank. On promotion a soldier takes the first step of the new rank, or the first step above his current pay if that is higher.

Military retirement

9% of basic pay is deducted and the state pays 13%. The pension is one thirty-fifth of the last basic pay for each year of service, i.e. last basic × months of service ÷ 420, capped at the last basic pay. A master sergeant with 30 years of service and a last basic of 12,255 gets a pension of about SAR 10,504.

Examples:
Sergeant, step 3: 5,760 + transport 500 = 6,260, minus a 518.40 deduction, so the net is SAR 5,741.60, or 6,241.60 from January 2027 with the SAR 500 allowance.
Private, step 1: 3,220 + 500 − 289.80 = SAR 3,430.20.
