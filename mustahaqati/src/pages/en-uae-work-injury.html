Calculate your compensation

Used for pay during treatment.
0 if fully recovered, 100 for total disability.

What the employer pays after a work injury

ItemRule
TreatmentAll costs until recovery or established disability, including hospital stay, surgery, prosthetics and transport
Pay during treatmentFull wage for up to 6 months, then half for another 6 months or until recovery, disability or death
Death24 months' basic wage, between AED 18,000 and 200,000, plus end-of-service gratuity
Total disabilitySame as death compensation
Partial disabilitySchedule percentage × death compensation
Reporting and paymentReport to the Ministry within 48 hours; pay within 10 days of the medical report setting the percentage

Examples:
45 days of treatment on a total salary of AED 9,000: 45 × 300 = AED 13,500 paid during treatment.
30% disability on a basic wage of AED 6,000: 24 × 6,000 = 144,000 × 30% = AED 43,200.
Death on a basic wage of AED 10,000: 240,000 is capped at AED 200,000; on AED 700, 16,800 is raised to 18,000.

The text can also be read as applying the AED 18,000 and 200,000 limits after the percentage for partial disability, so the calculator shows the difference where there is one. Results are estimates: the percentage is set by the competent medical authority. End-of-service gratuity is due on top; calculate it with the UAE gratuity calculator.
