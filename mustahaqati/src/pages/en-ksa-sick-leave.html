Sick leave tiers in Saudi Arabia

Days in the sick-leave yearPay
First 30 daysFull pay
Next 60 daysThree-quarters pay
Following 30 daysUnpaid

Example: actual wage SAR 9,000 and 45 sick days. Daily wage = SAR 300.
30 days on full pay = SAR 9,000; 15 days at three-quarters = 15 × 225 = SAR 3,375.
Sick pay = SAR 12,375 against SAR 13,500 of normal pay, a deduction of SAR 1,125.
