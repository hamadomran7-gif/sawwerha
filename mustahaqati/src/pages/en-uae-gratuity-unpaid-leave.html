Calculate your gratuity after deducting unpaid leave

Basic salary only, without allowances.
Deducted from your service, as the law requires.

The legal rule

Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 provides that days of absence from work without pay are not counted in the period of service. These days are deducted first, and the gratuity is then calculated on the remaining service with the usual rule: 21 days of basic salary for each of the first five years and 30 days for each year after that.

Example: a basic salary of AED 6,000 and service from 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2025 (five full years), including 90 days of unpaid leave.

Service after the deduction: 4 years and 9 months = 4.75 years.
Daily wage = 6,000 ÷ 30 = AED 200.
Gratuity = 4.75 × 21 × 200 = AED 19,950, instead of AED 21,000 without the unpaid leave.

How much does each unpaid month cost?

{{table:uae-unpaid-impact-en basic=6000 start=2021-01-01 end=2025-12-31 days=0,30,60,90,180,365 caption="5 years of service on a basic salary of AED 6,000"}}

If your service after the deduction is five years or less, each unpaid month reduces the gratuity by 1.75 days' pay (21 ÷ 12). Above five years, each month reduces it by 2.5 days' pay (30 ÷ 12), because the deduction effectively comes off the end of your service. In the example above: 3 months × 1.75 = 5.25 days × 200 = AED 1,050.

Before you take unpaid leave

  • Get written approval stating the number of days and the dates, as a reference when your gratuity is calculated.
  • If you are close to completing a year of service or the end of year five, check the effect of the days on those two thresholds first.
  • Check the effect on your annual leave balance and your ILOE unemployment insurance.
