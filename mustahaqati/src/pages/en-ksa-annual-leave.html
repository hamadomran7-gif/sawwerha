Annual leave rules in Saudi Arabia

  • Entitlement: at least 21 days a year on full pay, and 30 days after five consecutive years with the employer (Article 109).
  • Leaving work: unused days are paid at your last wage, and part years count pro rata (Article 111).
  • Daily rate: the monthly wage divided by 30.
  • Public holidays: the two Eids, National Day and Founding Day are separate from annual leave.

Example: wage SAR 8,000, six years of service (30 days a year), eight months into the leave year, five days used. Earned 20 days; remaining 15 days, worth SAR 4,000.
