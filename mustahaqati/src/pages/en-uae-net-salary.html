Example: an Emirati in the private sector registered after October 2023, basic 10,000 and housing 4,000.
The contribution salary is 14,000, so 11% = 1,540 is deducted and net pay is AED 12,460. The employer pays 12.5% = 1,750 and the government 2.5% = 350, so the employer's cost is 15,750.
If covered by Nafis Eshtirak, nothing is deducted and net pay stays at 14,000. If registered before October 2023, the deduction is 5% = 700 and net pay is 13,300.

GCC nationals working in the UAE contribute at their home country's rates through the insurance protection extension scheme; the employer pays no more than its UAE share and the employee pays the difference. In the DIFC, employers pay 5.83% of an expat's basic salary into the savings scheme (8.33% after 5 years) with no deduction from the employee.
