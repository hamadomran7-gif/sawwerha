How to work out your fuel cost

Full tank = tank size × price a litre. Monthly cost = (distance a month × litres per 100 km ÷ 100) × price a litre. A car that covers 2,000 km a month at 8 litres per 100 km needs 160 litres, which is QAR 312 with Premium 91 in November 2026, or QAR 3,744 a year.

How Qatar compares
