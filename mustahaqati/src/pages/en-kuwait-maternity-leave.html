Work out your maternity leave dates

As on the medical certificate.

Maternity leave rules in Kuwait's private sector

ItemRuleBasis
Length and pay70 paid days, not counted against other leaveArticle 24
ConditionThe birth must fall within the 70 days; no service conditionArticle 24
ExtensionUnpaid up to 4 months for childcare, at her request with the employer's approvalArticle 24
NursingTwo hours a day; the duration is set by ministry decisionArticle 25
ProtectionNo termination during the leave or an illness caused by pregnancy or birthArticle 24
NurseryA nursery for children under 4 where there are over 50 women or over 200 workersArticle 25
Paternity leaveNot provided by the law—

Example: expected date 10 December 2026, wage KWD 600 a month.
Starting on the birth date: leave until 17 February 2027, back on 18 February. Pay 70 × 20 = KWD 1,400.
With 4 months of unpaid childcare leave approved by the employer, she returns on 18 June 2027.
She may start before the birth as long as it falls within the 70 days, for example on 1 November 2026, ending 9 January 2027.

Days are calendar days and public holidays inside the leave do not extend it. Some guides split the leave "30 before and 40 after", but the law has no such split. Pay is the usual full wage, paid by the employer.

The government sector differs: two months on full salary provided the birth falls within them, then maternity care leave for Kuwaiti women (the first month on full salary and the next three on half salary if requested). For other leave use the Kuwait leave calculator.
