Work out your family residence cost

Family residence fees in Bahrain

ItemAmount (BHD)When
Dependent residence permit90For two years, issue or renewal
Admin fee (LMRA)5Each application
Smart card10At issue
Private health insuranceInsurer's priceMandatory for new applications since 1 Sep 2026

Example: a wife and two children: permits 3 × 95 = 285 and cards 30 = BHD 315 for two years plus insurance, then about BHD 142.5 a year for renewal.

Dependent applications go through the employer's account, with a net salary of BHD 400 required, or 1,000 for parents and children over 24 through the Nationality, Passports and Residence Affairs. The mandatory insurance must cover at least the worker's permit period.

For your net pay after contributions use the Bahrain net salary calculator.
