Calculate your maximum support

Gross salary with allowances before deductions, and all income of the household and dependents. Loans are not deducted.
SAR 4 million for a family of 6 and SAR 1,371,200 for an independent (property, vehicles, cash and investments).

How Citizen's Account support is calculated

The program calculates support from household income, household size and the dependents' ages. The head of household or an independent weighs 100% of an individual's entitlement, a dependent aged 18 or over 50%, and a dependent under 18 30%, according to a 2017 circular. The full individual entitlement has been SAR 720 since July 2022, giving:

Head of household or independent100%720
Dependent aged 18 or over (including the wife)50%360
Dependent under 1830%216
Household cap (head + 2 over 18 + 3 under 18)290%2,088

The weights come from a circular reported by Okaz and the 720 from a 2022 program statement; we found no recent official document stating the same amount, so these figures are estimates. The reference-family cap and the SAR 20,000 maximum income were published officially in Umm Al-Qura in October 2023.

Exemption threshold and income cutoff

  • Income below your household's exemption threshold: you get the full support (the maximum in the calculator).
  • Between the exemption threshold and the cutoff: support falls as income rises; the taper rate is not published.
  • Above the cutoff: no support. The cutoff is at most SAR 20,000 for any household, and lower for smaller households.

The program publishes neither the exemption threshold nor the cutoff for each composition; its official estimator shows both for each case. So our calculator gives the maximum your support cannot exceed, not the exact amount. In the October 2026 payment, 72% of eligible people received support, averaging about SAR 1,469 per family.

Eligibility

  • The beneficiary must be a Saudi living in the Kingdom and not in prison or a government shelter. Holders of mobility cards, the non-Saudi husband of a Saudi woman and her children, and the non-Saudi wife of a Saudi man are exempt from the nationality condition.
  • An independent must be 18 or over, unmarried, and have documented independent housing (a lease or title deed) matching the national address, with a verification visit.
  • Financial capacity: SAR 4 million for a family of 6 and SAR 1,371,200 for an independent, covering property, vehicles, cash, investments and the number of domestic workers.
  • Entitlement starts from the Gregorian month after registration is complete.

Additional support until the end of 2026

The additional support began in July 2022, raising an independent's entitlement from SAR 417 to 720, and has been extended several times, most recently to the end of 2026 by a royal order on 2 December 2025. No decision for 2027 had been issued by 3 November 2026, and there is no text on the amount if it is not extended.

Support is paid on the 10th of each Gregorian month; if that is a Friday it moves to Thursday, and if a Saturday to Sunday. The November and December 2026 payments are numbers 108 and 109, the latter being the last covered by the current extension. For a detailed countdown see Citizen's Account payment date.

Examples:
Head, wife, a 19-year-old son and 3 children: 720 + 2 × 360 + 3 × 216 = SAR 2,088 maximum, which is the household cap.
Head, wife and 4 children: 720 + 360 + 4 × 216 = SAR 1,944.
Head, wife and 6 children: 2,376 before the cap and 2,088 after it.
A household with income of SAR 20,500: no support regardless of size.
