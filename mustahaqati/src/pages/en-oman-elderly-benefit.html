Elderly benefit and the income test

The elderly benefit is OMR 115 a month for Omani residents from the month they turn 60. From April 2026 it depends on income: full when monthly income is OMR 1,020 or less, reduced gradually between 1,020 and 1,250, and not paid from 1,250. Neither the reduction formula nor the definition of income has been announced, so the calculator estimates the reduction on a straight line (115 minus half of the income above 1,020), which reaches zero exactly at 1,250.

Age and combining

  • Age: 60, rising one year every 7 years from 2024 up to 65 and then reviewed every 5 years, so the first rise should come no earlier than 2031 on our reading.
  • With the disability benefit: the higher is paid (130).
  • With the widow benefit: the elderly benefit is paid instead, since the widow benefit is for women below the elderly age.
  • With pensions: combined, subject to the income test from April 2026.

Payment and registration

Benefits are paid monthly no later than the end of the month they are due for, with no fixed day announced, for the full month whatever the entitlement date, and up to 3 months retroactively. Register by confirming your details in your personal account on the Fund's platform or with approved service providers. Work out household support with the family income support calculator.

Examples:
An Omani aged 63 with a pension of 600: the full OMR 115, for total income of 715.
An Omani with income of 1,100 after April 2026: between the thresholds, a straight-line estimate of OMR 75 (unofficial).
An Omani with income of 1,300: no elderly benefit, but a person with a disability gets the OMR 130 disability benefit.
