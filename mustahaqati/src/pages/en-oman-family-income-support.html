Calculate your family income support

Spouses, non-working sons up to 26 and unmarried daughters under 40.
Wages, pensions and asset income, excluding the child and disability benefits.
Each counts as two members.
OMR 115 imputed income each (not elderly, disabled or sons under 26).

Target value by household size

{{table:om-fis-targets lang=en}}

Article 40 of the Social Protection Law sets the target at OMR 115 for a one-person household, multiplied by the square root of the number of members, with the Fund paying the difference between the target and household income. The regulation says the benefit, once calculated and confirmed, must not be less than OMR 25, or 15 for a one-person household, which we read as raising the amount to that minimum.

Counted income and imputed income

  • Counted: wages, asset income at no less than an imputed return, pensions, allowances and regular payments, and the orphan and widow benefit (except one orphan's benefit in a household with two or more orphans).
  • Not counted: the child benefit, the disability benefit, the elderly benefit of one person in a household of two or more, and child maintenance for widows and divorcees since 2026.
  • Imputed income for men: OMR 115 for a man able to work but not working, except elderly or disabled men, those unable to work and non-working sons up to 26.
  • Imputed income for women: OMR 115 with exemptions including elderly and disabled women, widows, divorced mothers with custody and wives of elderly or disabled men, and 35 for unmarried, widowed or divorced women without children; the latter was abolished for four groups from February 2026, raising their maximum from 80 to 115.

Child benefit

OMR 10 a month for each Omani child under 18 from the month of birth, paid into a bank account in the child's name. From February 2026 it doubles to OMR 20 for school students in families receiving income support during the school year, without applying. A child entitled to the disability benefit (130) gets the higher amount.

Conditions and procedure

  • At least one member must be Omani, the household must live in Oman, and its income must be below the target.
  • Asset limits: two residential properties worth 30,000 or more for households of up to two, four worth 60,000 or more for larger households, or more than two domestic workers other than a carer.
  • The Fund decides within 30 days; the benefit is due from the application date and paid to the head of household's account, and you can reapply after a change in circumstances or 6 months after a refusal.

Examples:
A household of 8 with income of 200: target 325.27, so the benefit is OMR 125.27, the example published by Oman Daily.
A household of 6 with a father earning 200 and 4 children, 3 of them students: income support 281.69 − 200 = 81.69, child benefit 3 × 20 + 10 = 70, total OMR 151.69 in the school year.
A household of 5 with income of 250: the gap of 7.15 is raised to the OMR 25 minimum.
