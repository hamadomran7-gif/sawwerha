Work out your company's target

Skill levels 1–5, a qualification above secondary school and a salary of AED 4,000 or more.
In skilled jobs, paid AED 6,000 or more, registered for pension and WPS.
The resolution requires raising your "current" rate, so the table reading adds the growth on top of them.

The two readings match when the skilled headcount is a multiple of 50 and differ otherwise, because the table counts "part of 50" as a whole Emirati. We found no text on rounding the half-year 1% step, so we assume half the yearly increase, rounded up.

No contribution is charged if the company restores its rate within two months of a drop (when an Emirati resigns, for example). Companies whose Emiratis make up 70% or more of their total workforce were exempted from contributions in a January 2023 amendment. The Emiratisation Partners Club gives up to 80% off ministry fees and priority in government procurement to companies that far exceed their targets.

Examples:
A company with 80 workers, 60 of them in skilled jobs, and 8 Emiratis: the table requires two Emiratis a year, so 10 by the end of 2026, a shortfall of 2 and a contribution of AED 240,000. At a flat 10% it needs 6, so no shortfall.
A company with 900 workers, 600 of them skilled, and 50 Emiratis: both readings require 60 by the end of 2026, a shortfall of 10 and a contribution of AED 1,200,000.
A construction company with 30 workers and one Emirati at the end of 2025: it needed 2, so a shortfall of 1 and a contribution of AED 108,000, payable from January 2026 and allowed in monthly instalments.

This is an estimate that assumes your skilled headcount has not changed since 2022; the binding figure is what the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation shows for your company in its system. It covers mainland companies registered with the ministry, not free zones. Private healthcare has an extra rule: half of the annual target must be in specialised health jobs, assessed from 2027.
