How sick leave is paid in the UAE

Article 31 of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021 gives a worker who has finished probation up to 90 days of sick leave, consecutive or intermittent, for each year of service, in three tiers:

DaysPay
First 15 daysFull pay
Next 30 daysHalf pay
Following 45 daysUnpaid

The daily wage is the monthly wage divided by 30. Days are added up within the same year of service, whether as one long absence or separate days.

Example: basic salary AED 8,000 and allowances AED 4,000 (gross AED 12,000), 20 sick days after probation.

Daily wage = 12,000 ÷ 30 = AED 400.
First 15 days on full pay = 15 × 400 = AED 6,000.
Next 5 days on half pay = 5 × 200 = AED 1,000.
Sick leave pay = AED 7,000 instead of AED 8,000, a deduction of AED 1,000.
On basic salary alone the pay would be only AED 4,666.67.

Conditions to know

  • Notice: within 3 working days, with a medical report.
  • Probation: no paid sick leave; unpaid sick leave may be granted.
  • Loss of pay: illness caused by misconduct or by breaking safety instructions (executive regulations, Article 20).
  • After the 90 days: the employer may end your service if you cannot return, and must pay all your dues.
