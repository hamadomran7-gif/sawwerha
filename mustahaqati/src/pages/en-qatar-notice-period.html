Work out your notice and pay in lieu

Pay in lieu uses basic pay since the 2020 amendment.

Notice rules in Qatar

ItemRule
Notice periodOne month in the first two years of service, two months after (Article 49 since 2020)
Who must give itBoth sides, for every pay basis; the contract may set a longer period
Pay in lieuBasic wage for the notice period or the part not served
During noticeFull pay for those who keep working; Qataris may take reasonable time off to look for work (Article 50)
ProbationUp to 6 months: employer one month's notice; a worker moving to another employer one month; a worker leaving Qatar the agreed period, up to two months
Leaving without noticePay in lieu; an expatriate may be barred from a work permit for a year; dues are paid within 7 days

Example: Basic salary QAR 6,000 and 3 years of service: two months' notice. If the employer ends the contract immediately, pay in lieu is QAR 12,000. If the worker resigns and leaves after one of the two months, the worker owes QAR 6,000.
