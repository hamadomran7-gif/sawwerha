Breaker capacityMonthly fee

Example from the announcement: a home using 1,000 kWh a month on a breaker under 100 amps: consumption 1,000 × 0.21 = SAR 210 + fixed fee SAR 70 = SAR 280 before VAT, or SAR 322 with 15% VAT. Under the current tariff, the consumption charge was SAR 180.

Prices are from the Saudi Energy Company announcement as reported in the press on 25 October 2026; your official bill is the reference, and it may differ depending on how tiers are pro-rated over the billing days. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
