Work out your maternity leave dates

As on the medical certificate.

Maternity leave rules in Bahrain's private sector

ItemRuleBasis
Length and pay60 paid days covering before and after the birthArticle 32(a)
Unpaid15 more days as of rightArticle 32(a)
Ban on work40 days after the birthArticle 32(b)
NursingTwo hours a day until 6 months, then one hour until the first birthdayArticle 35
ChildcareUnpaid up to 6 months at a time, 3 times, for a child under 6Article 34
ProtectionNo dismissal because of marriage or during maternity leaveArticle 33
Paternity leaveOne paid dayArticle 63(b)

Example: expected date 10 December 2026, total wage BHD 600.
Starting on the birth date: leave until 7 February 2027, back on 8 February. Pay 60 × 20 = BHD 1,200.
With the 15 unpaid days it ends on 22 February, back on 23 February. The ban on work runs until 18 January 2027.
Nursing: two hours a day from the return until 9 June 2027, then one hour until 9 December 2027.

There is no service condition, and Bahrain has no insurance branch that reimburses employers for maternity pay. The law defines the wage as basic plus allowances, so leave pay is most likely the total wage. The employer sets the timing of the nursing breaks according to the worker's circumstances and the needs of the work.

Government sector: 60 days on full salary including allowances, which may start 15 days before the birth, and two nursing hours until the child turns two. For other leave use the Bahrain leave calculator.
