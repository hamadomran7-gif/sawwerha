Calculate your gratuity

Basic salary only, without allowances.

Two periods, two rules

  • From 31 July 2023: one month's basic salary for each year of service (Article 61 of the current law).
  • Before 31 July 2023: 15 days' basic salary for each of the first three years, then a month for each year after (the previous law), as the Ministry of Labour explains.

Example: basic salary OMR 600, from 1 January 2020 to 31 October 2026. About 3.58 years before 31 July 2023 give 0.5 × 3 + 0.58 = 2.08 months, and about 3.25 years after give 3.25 months, for a total of about 5.33 months, or roughly OMR 3,200.
