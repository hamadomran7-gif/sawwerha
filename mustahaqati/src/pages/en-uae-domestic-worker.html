Domestic worker rights in the UAE

RightEntitlement
Gratuity14 days of basic wage per year after one year of service (ministry announcement)
Annual leave30 paid days a year, paid in advance; 2 days per month between 6 months and a year; a ticket every two years
Sick leave30 days a year: 15 on full pay, then 15 on half pay
RestAt least 12 hours a day, 8 of them consecutive, and a paid weekly day off
ProbationUp to 6 months
Final duesWithin 10 days of the contract ending

Example: basic wage AED 1,500 (daily 50), 3 years and 4 months of service and 10 unused leave days: gratuity 50 × 14 × 3.33 = 2,333.33 plus leave pay 500, a total of AED 2,833.33, plus the return ticket.
