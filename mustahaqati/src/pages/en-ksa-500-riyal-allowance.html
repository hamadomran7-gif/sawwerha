Are you covered? How much does it add?

The allowance is split equally among them.

What was announced

On 25 October 2026 (14 Jumada I 1448H) King Salman, on the Crown Prince and Prime Minister's proposal, directed supportive measures for citizens alongside new electricity and water tariffs from 1 January 2027:

  • A SAR 500 monthly allowance increase for civil and military employees on civil service scales and operating programmes, retirees, and family members of a deceased insured person or retiree.
  • SAR 500 monthly support for families on social security, under its rules.
  • A higher Nitaqat threshold for counting a Saudi worker fully: SAR 5,000 and then 6,000, in the private sector.

Payment details

  • Retirees: GOSI said it will be paid automatically from 1 January 2027 to pensioners in all schemes, disability pensioners and survivors, with one allowance for anyone with several pensions, split equally among a deceased member's survivors, and not to Saned beneficiaries. Pensions are paid on the 1st of each month.
  • Employees and military staff: the press reported a 1 January 2027 start and that it is not added to basic salary, so it is expected with the January salary on the 27th.
  • Social security families: no start date, and no word on whether it is per family or per person, as of 3 November 2026.

How much do bills rise in 2027?

Residential electricity rises from 18 and 30 halalas to 21 and 33 halalas per kWh, plus a fixed charge of SAR 70 to 100 by breaker size, and water tiers change with new fixed charges. The table is an estimate including 15% VAT and excluding today's unpublished meter fee:

Examples:
A civil employee earning 9,000: income becomes 9,500 from January 2027, SAR 6,000 more a year, with no change to the pension deduction since the allowance is outside basic pay.
Three survivors of a deceased member: the allowance is split, so each gets SAR 166.67 a month.
A private-sector employee or a Saned beneficiary: not covered.
