Work out your Darb cost

7–9 AM and 3–7 PM. Crossings at other times are free.

Current Darb rules

  • Fee: AED 4 per crossing at peak times.
  • Peak: 7–9 AM and 3–7 PM, Monday to Saturday (the evening window was 5–7 PM before September 2025).
  • Free: all other times, Sundays and official public holidays.
  • Caps: the daily cap (previously AED 16) and the monthly cap for private vehicles were removed on 1 September 2025.

Example: an employee crossing two gates in the morning and two in the evening, five days a week: 4 × AED 4 = AED 16 a day, AED 80 a week and about AED 346 a month.
