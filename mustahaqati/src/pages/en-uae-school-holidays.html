Dubai and Sharjah private schools

KHDA required Dubai private schools to follow the unified calendar, so the October break moved to 12–16 October. Sharjah private schools have their own calendar: winter break 14 December – 3 January, spring break 5–8 April and the year ending on 1 July.

{{table:school-calendar country=ae variant=sharjah-private lang=en}}

The year has 185 school days in three terms, and the last week of each term is for final assessments with attendance required. Public holidays are on UAE public holidays.
