How many days to the holiday?

MOE school holidays 2026–2027

The ministry calendar gives the National Day holiday from Wednesday 2 to Friday 4 December 2026, for public and private schools that follow the MOE curriculum (Academic Calendar 2026–2027, PDF).

Dubai private schools

KHDA required Dubai private schools to follow the unified calendar, so the October break moved to 12–16 October; the spring break runs from Monday 5 to Friday 9 April 2027 with return on Monday 12 April, and the last school day is Friday 2 July 2027 (KHDA calendar for Dubai private schools). The Eid Al Etihad holiday is Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 December 2026, the official holiday for the public and private sectors (UAE Government portal); Friday 4 December is part of the MOE schools' break, but the KHDA calendar does not list it, so your school's circular decides it.

Sharjah private schools

Sharjah private schools have their own calendar: winter break 14 December – 3 January, spring break from Monday 5 to Thursday 8 April 2027 with return on 12 April, and the year ending on Thursday 1 July 2027. The Eid Al Etihad holiday is Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 December 2026, the official holiday (UAE Government portal); your school's circular decides Friday 4 December.

{{table:school-calendar country=ae variant=sharjah-private lang=en}}

The year has 185 school days in three terms, and the last week of each term is for final assessments with attendance required. Public holidays are on UAE public holidays.
