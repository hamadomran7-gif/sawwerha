The tables are generated with the same rules as the GOSI pension calculator and assume you have reached the statutory retirement age and meet the pension conditions.

Previous system (insured before 3 July 2024)

{{table:ksa-pension-grid lang=en}}

Pension = average wage of the last 24 months × months of contribution ÷ 480, up to 100%. Asterisk: raised to the SAR 1,983.75 minimum.

New system (first insured from 3 July 2024)

{{table:ksa-pension-grid system=new lang=en}}

Pension = 2.25% × average of the highest wages over 180 months × years of contribution, at age 65 with at least 180 months. Asterisk: raised to the graduated minimum of SAR 2,000 to 4,000.

What the table leaves out

  • The contributory wage is basic salary plus housing allowance only, capped at SAR 45,000.
  • Early retirement under the new system is reduced by 3% for each year, and deferring past 65 adds 3% for each year.
  • Older members in the transitional group have a retirement age between 58 and 65 depending on their age in July 2024, with the same formula.
