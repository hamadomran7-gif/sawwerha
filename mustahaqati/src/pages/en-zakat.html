Calculate your zakat

How zakat on wealth is calculated

  1. Add up your zakatable wealth: cash, savings, gold and silver at today's value, trade goods and money you expect to collect.
  2. Compare it with the nisab: 85 grams of pure gold or 595 grams of silver at today's prices.
  3. If it reaches the nisab and a lunar year has passed, pay 2.5% (one fortieth) of the whole amount.

Example: savings of 60,000 and 50 grams of 21-carat gold, with 24-carat gold at 400 a gram. Pure gold = 50 × 21 ÷ 24 = 43.75 g, worth 17,500. Total 77,500, above the gold nisab of 34,000 (85 × 400). Zakat = 77,500 ÷ 40 = 1,937.50.
