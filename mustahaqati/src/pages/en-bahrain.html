Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service gratuityHalf a month's wage for each of the first 3 years, then a month, on basic plus social allowance. For service since 1 March 2024 it is paid by the Social Insurance Organization.Article 116 and Decision 109/2023
Final settlementGratuity with the SIO share, leave pay, and 30 days' notice.Law 36/2012 Articles 58, 99 and 116
Annual leave30 days at 2.5 days a month, paid on basic plus social allowance.Articles 58 and 59
Sick leaveAfter 3 months: 15 paid days, then 20 on half pay, then 20 unpaid, with balances accumulating up to 240 days.Articles 65 and 66
Maternity leave60 paid days and 15 unpaid, a 40-day ban on work after the birth, two nursing hours until 6 months then one, and one day for fathers.Articles 32–35 and 63
Pension (Bahrainis)2% per year of the last 5 years' average wage, at 60 with 180 months; early retirement with 240 months at a reduction, otherwise a lump sum.Social Insurance Law as amended by Law 14/2022
Net salary after SIO7% + 1% unemployment for Bahrainis, 15% + 3% injury from the employer in 2026 rising to 17%, and 1% for expats with a 4.2% or 8.4% gratuity contribution from the employer.Law 14/2022
Civil service salary scale4 official tables (general, shift, executive and educational) with 16 ranks per grade, BHD 20 transport, the 85 or 100 living allowance, and a 7% pension on basic.Civil Service Bureau tables and Regulation 77/2013
Family residence costPermit BHD 90 for two years, BHD 5 fee and BHD 10 card, a BHD 400 salary rule, and mandatory insurance for new applications since September 2026.Labour Market Regulatory Authority
Unemployment insuranceCompensation of 60% of the average wage between BHD 200 and 1,000 for 9 months after 12 months of contributions; first-time job seekers get 200 (graduates) or 150; nothing on resignation.Decree-Law 78/2006 and Law 4/2019
OvertimePay + 25% by day and + 50% at night (7 pm–7 am); rest days and holidays + 150% or a substitute day at the worker's choice.Articles 54, 57 and 64
Notice period30 days for both sides; pay in lieu on basic plus social allowance.Article 99
Public holidays14 days including National Day on 16–17 December; work on them: wage + 150%, or a day off at the worker's choice.Article 64 and Decision 1/2013
School holidays 2026–2027National Day 16–17 Dec, mid-year 17–28 Jan, summer from 1 Jul 2027.Education Ministry calendar
