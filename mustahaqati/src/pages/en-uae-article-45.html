Calculate your gratuity on leaving

Basic salary only, without allowances.
Deducted from your service, as the law requires.

The 4 cases in Article 45

CaseCondition
1. The employer breaches its obligations under the contract, the law or its implementing decisionsNotify the ministry 14 working days before leaving, and the employer fails to remedy the breach despite the ministry's notice
2. Assault by the employer or its legal representative, or violence or harassment during workReport to the authorities and the ministry within 5 working days of being able to
3. A serious danger at the workplace threatening the worker's safety or healthThe employer knew of it and took no steps to remove it, per the executive regulations
4. Being assigned work fundamentally different from what was agreed, without written consentExcept in cases of necessity under Article 12

What you keep

  • Full end-of-service gratuity after one year: 21 days of basic salary for each of the first five years, then 30 days.
  • No notice pay owed to the employer.
  • Your outstanding pay and cash for unused annual leave.

Example (the calculator above): basic salary AED 6,000 and 4 years 7 months of service; the employer did not pay wages.

The worker notified the ministry, 14 working days passed without the employer fixing it, and the worker left.
Gratuity = 4.58 years × 21 days × AED 200 = about AED 19,250, with no notice pay owed.

Mind the deadlines

The 14-day period in the first case is in working days, not calendar days. Leaving before the conditions are met may be treated as leaving without notice, making you liable for notice pay. See also Article 44 for when an employer may dismiss without notice. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
