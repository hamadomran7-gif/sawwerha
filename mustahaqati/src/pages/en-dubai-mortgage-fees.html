These are maximums; a bank may lend less depending on your income, age and the property. The down payment is the difference between the price and the loan.

Cash fees on purchase day

  • Transfer fee: 4% of the price to the Dubai Land Department.
  • Title deed: AED 580 plus AED 20 in knowledge and innovation fees.
  • Registration trustee: AED 2,000 (under AED 500,000) or AED 4,000 (above), plus 5% VAT.
  • Mortgage registration: 0.25% of the loan plus AED 290.
  • Bank valuation: about AED 2,500 to 3,500.
  • Bank arrangement fee: usually up to 1% of the loan.
  • Agent commission: 2% of the price plus 5% VAT.
  • Developer NOC: AED 500 to 5,000 depending on the developer (not included in the calculator).

Example: an apartment at AED 1,500,000, resident buyer, first home, 80% financing at 4.5% over 25 years, through an agent.

Down payment AED 300,000. Fees: DLD 60,000 + 600 + trustee 4,200 + mortgage registration 3,290 + valuation 3,000 + bank 12,000 + agent 31,500 ≈ AED 114,590.
Cash needed ≈ AED 414,590, and the monthly payment ≈ AED 6,670.
