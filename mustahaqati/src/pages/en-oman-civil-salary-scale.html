Calculate your salary from the grade scale

One increment each 1 January after six months of service, including any granted at appointment for experience.

Oman's unified grades and salaries scale

{{table:om-civil-scale lang=en}}

The scale is the annex to Royal Decree 78/2013 and has applied to Omani civil employees in the state administrative apparatus since 1 January 2014. Each grade has a starting basic and a fixed annual increment with no maximum in the table, and the allowances are fixed amounts per grade, not percentages of basic pay, so they do not change with increments.

Appointment grade by qualification

  • PhD: grade 9, OMR 1,035 in total.
  • Master's: grade 10, OMR 910.
  • Bachelor's: grade 11, OMR 785.

Civil Service Council Decision 3/2020 placed qualified appointees one grade lower from 17 May 2020, cutting the PhD total by 23% and the bachelor's by 14%. An appointee also gets one increment of the grade for each year of relevant experience above the requirement (Article 35).

Annual and incentive increments

  • Annual: on 1 January each year, equal to the grade's increment, after at least six months of service, withheld in the year after a "weak" rating. In 2021 it was paid in full to those with totals under OMR 900 and half to those above; we found no reduction since.
  • Incentive: one or two increments a year, up to four in the grade, for those whose latest rating is "very good" or above (Article 37).

Promotion

A promoted employee gets the higher of the new grade's starting basic or current pay plus one increment of the new grade, and the annual increment date does not change (Article 44). Promotion requires at least three years. The 2026 promotion system has three tracks (exceptional performance, preferential selection by points, and retention), with decisions effective on 2 January and 2 July and an OMR 10 million budget for 2026.

Deductions

Since 1 January 2024, 7.5% is deducted for the Social Protection Fund on basic pay plus all allowances, capped at OMR 3,000 a month, and 0.5% for job security with no cap. The employer pays 11%, 0.5% job security and 1% each for work injury, maternity and sick leave. For any other salary, use the Oman net salary calculator.

Examples:
Bachelor's holder appointed at grade 11: 400 + allowances 385 = 785, deductions 58.875 + 3.925 = 62.800, net OMR 722.200.
The same employee after three increments: basic 445, total 830, net OMR 763.600.
Promotion to grade 10 after five increments: the higher of 460 and 475 + 15 = 490 basic, total 940.
Grade 1: total 3,780, pension on the 3,000 cap = 225, job security 18.900, net OMR 3,536.100.
