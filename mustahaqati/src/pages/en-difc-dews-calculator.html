How the DEWS contribution is calculated

Article 66(7) of the DIFC Employment Law requires the employer to pay, every month, into a qualifying scheme for each employee who is not exempted, at least the "Core Benefits":

  1. 5.83% of the monthly basic wage for the first five years of the employee's service, including service before the scheme start date.
  2. 8.33% of the monthly basic wage for each additional year of service.

The monthly basic wage is the annual wage excluding allowances, divided by 12, and for this calculation it cannot be less than 50% of the monthly wage (Article 66(8)(b)). Each month's contribution must be paid by the 21st day of the following month, otherwise the employer is in breach (Article 66(17)).

Worked example: basic wage AED 25,000, ten years of service that started after 1 February 2020.

First five years: 25,000 × 5.83% = AED 1,457.50 a month, × 60 months = AED 87,450.
Next five years: 25,000 × 8.33% = AED 2,082.50 a month, × 60 months = AED 124,950.
Total = AED 212,400 as a minimum, before investment returns and plan fees.

When the plan starts for you

  • Employed in the DIFC on 1 February 2020: from that date.
  • Employed on or after 1 February 2020: from your start date.
  • Your employer must register you with a qualifying scheme within two months of that date, and contributions are due back to it (Article 66(11)).

Gratuity for service before 1 February 2020

An employee who is not registered with the GPSSA and has at least one year of continuous service is entitled on termination to a gratuity for service before the scheme start date (Article 66(1) and (2)):

  • 21 days of basic wage for each of the first five years and 30 days for each additional year, capped at two years' wage.
  • The daily rate is the annual basic wage divided by 365, and the wages used are those at the termination date (Article 66(3)).
  • The employer may transfer this amount to the plan; with the employee's prior written consent, that ends the obligation to pay it on termination (Article 66(6)).

This is the DIFC rule only. Employees outside the DIFC under the federal labour law can use the UAE gratuity calculator.

UAE and GCC nationals

They are registered with the GPSSA and are not entitled to a gratuity (Article 65(1) and (2)). If the employer's GPSSA contribution is below the contribution they would have received in the plan, they are entitled to a top-up of the difference into a qualifying scheme, provided the monthly top-up obligation is AED 1,000 or more (Article 65(3)). If the employer does not pay

Schedule 2 of the law sets a maximum fine of USD 2,000 for each contravention in respect of each employee for breaches of Article 66, including late contributions.
