What we don't collect

The calculators on this site run entirely in your browser. The amounts and dates you enter are not sent to our servers or to any third party. No sign-up or email address is needed to use any tool.

Local storage

For convenience, your browser may save the last values you entered in each calculator in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device, and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

Cookies and advertising

We may use advertising networks such as Google AdSense to cover the site's costs. These networks use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised Google ads in Ad Settings, or disable cookies in your browser.

Analytics

We may use aggregated analytics tools to see which pages are used most, without identifying visitors. We do not sell any data or share it for marketing.

External links

Links to official websites and sources are outside our control, and each site has its own policy.

Children

The site is intended for adult employees and employers, and we do not knowingly collect data about children.
