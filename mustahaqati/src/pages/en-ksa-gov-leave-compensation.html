Calculate the payment

Without allowances.
From your balance statement in your employer's system.
Leave at 0 if you have no older balance.

The encashment rule (Article 133)

Reason service endedDays paid
Normal or early retirement, resignation and dismissalUp to 72 days of the balance
Those with a balance earned before 20 March 2018Older balance + newer balance up to 72, with a total of up to 180 days
Death (paid to heirs) or health incapacityThe full balance, with no limit

Amount = last basic salary ÷ 30 × the days paid, without allowances. Dividing by 30 is our reading of the definition of a month in Article 1. Sick and exceptional leave balances are not paid.

Don't lose your balance before retiring

Anything above 72 days is lost on retirement or resignation. The regulation allows up to 108 days of leave a year, or 144 days for those with 25 years of pensionable service or aged 50, so plan to use the excess before your last working day. A balance also lapses if not requested within 3 years of the end of the year it was earned (a fourth year with the employer's approval).

Examples with a basic salary of SAR 12,000 (daily 400):
Retirement with 150 days all earned after 2018: 72 × 400 = SAR 28,800, and 78 days are lost.
Resignation with 100 older days + 50 newer: 150 × 400 = SAR 60,000.
Death with a balance of 150 days: the full balance = SAR 60,000 to the heirs.

Teachers take the summer holiday as their ordinary leave, so a balance does not usually build up, and the final year's balance is counted from the return date to retirement. Employees under the Labour Law in government bodies follow the private-sector calculators. For your pension, see the civil retirement calculator.
