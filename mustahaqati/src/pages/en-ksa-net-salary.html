Nationality and system

What is deducted from your salary

  • Saudi, subscribed before 3 July 2024: 9% pension + 0.75% SANED = 9.75% of basic plus housing.
  • Saudi, new subscriber: a pension rate that started at 9% and rises by half a point each July until 11%, plus 0.75% SANED.
  • Non-Saudi: nothing. The employer pays 2% for occupational hazards.

Example: a Saudi employee subscribed before July 2024 with basic SAR 10,000, housing SAR 2,500 and transport SAR 1,000. Contributory wage SAR 12,500; deduction 9.75% = SAR 1,218.75. Net salary = 13,500 − 1,218.75 = SAR 12,281.25.
