A government subsidies committee sets prices every quarter. We found no published decision for Q4 2026 (October–December), so the Ultra 98 figure is the latest announced. Kuwait has no VAT.

How to work out your fuel cost

Full tank = tank size × price a litre. Monthly cost = (distance a month × litres per 100 km ÷ 100) × price. A car covering 2,000 km a month at 8 litres per 100 km needs 160 litres: KD 13.600 with Premium 91, or KD 163.200 a year.
