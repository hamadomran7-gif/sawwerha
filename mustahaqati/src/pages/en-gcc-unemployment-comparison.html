What would you get if you lost your job?

UAE: basic. Saudi Arabia: basic plus housing. Oman: two-year average. Bahrain: 12-month average.

What they share: nothing for resignation or disciplinary dismissal, and most schemes require prior contributions (12 consecutive months in the UAE and Bahrain, 12 months within 36 for SANED) and registering as a job seeker within a deadline. Payment stops when you find work or refuse a suitable offer.

Expats are covered by only two schemes: the UAE's unemployment insurance, which is mandatory with fines for non-subscription, and Bahrain's. Emiratis may receive both UAE unemployment insurance and Nafis support; sources do not settle whether one is deducted from the other.

We have not added Qatar and Kuwait yet because we have not verified their unemployment schemes; Kuwaitis in the private sector receive national labour support while in work. For what your employer owes you at the end of service, see the GCC end-of-service comparison.
