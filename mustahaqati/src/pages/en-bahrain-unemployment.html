Compensation and allowance after the 2019 amendment

ItemCompensation (insured unemployed)Allowance (first-time job seeker)
Amount60% of the 12-month average wage200 for graduates, 150 for others
LimitsBHD 200 to 1,000—
Duration9 months, and 9 in every 24 months9 months within 12 months
Contributions required12 consecutive months for a first claimNone
NationalityBahrainis and legally resident expatriatesBahrainis only

Examples:
Average BHD 1,200: 720 × 9 = BHD 6,480.
Average 2,500: 60% = 1,500, capped at 1,000: 1,000 × 9 = BHD 9,000.
Average 300: 60% = 180, raised to the minimum: 200 × 9 = BHD 1,800.
Someone dismissed after only 8 months of contributions gets no compensation, but the allowance if Bahraini.

A second claim needs 12 months of contributions within 18, a third 18 within 24, and later claims 36 within 48. The right lapses if a suitable job is refused twice without good reason. For your end-of-service award, use the Bahrain end-of-service calculator.
