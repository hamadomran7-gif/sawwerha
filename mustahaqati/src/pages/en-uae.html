Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service gratuity21 days' basic salary for each of the first five years, then 30 days for each year after, capped at two years' pay, after one year of service.Article 51
Final settlementGratuity and leave pay (on basic), notice pay and unpaid days (on the full wage), paid within 14 days.Articles 29, 43, 51, 53
Domestic workers14 days' gratuity per year after one year, 30 days' annual leave and a ticket every two years, and a refund of recruitment costs for the remaining months.Decree-Law 9/2022
Annual leave30 days per year of service, and 2 days a month if service is over six months but under a year. Unused days are paid when the contract ends.Article 29
OvertimeThe hourly wage plus at least 25%, rising to 50% for overtime between 10 pm and 4 am or on a rest day.Articles 17–21
Notice payA notice period set by the contract between 30 and 90 days; whoever does not observe it pays the wage for the unobserved period on the last full wage.Articles 43 and 9
Sick leaveAfter probation: 90 days per year of service, the first 15 on full pay, then 30 on half pay, then 45 unpaid.Article 31
Maternity leave60 days: 45 on full pay and 15 on half pay, plus 5 working days of parental leave for either parent within 6 months.Articles 30 and 32
Bereavement, parental and study leaveBereavement 5 days for a spouse and 3 for a parent, child, sibling, grandchild or grandparent; parental leave 5 working days; study leave 10 working days after two years.Article 32
Ramadan working hoursTwo hours less a day for all workers, with no deduction from pay.Executive Regulations, Art. 15
Unemployment insurance (ILOE)AED 5 a month for basic salaries up to AED 16,000 and AED 10 above, paying 60% of basic salary for up to three months.Decree-Law 13/2022
Nafis job loss supportAED 7,000 a month, 4,500 for a non-working spouse and 2,400, 2,400 and 1,600 for children, minus other income, for 6 consecutive and 12 total months.Nafis programme
Dismissal without notice10 cases in Article 44 after a written investigation and a reasoned decision; gratuity is not forfeited.Articles 44 and 39
Leaving without notice4 cases in Article 45, including employer breaches after notifying the Ministry 14 working days in advance.Article 45
Nationals' pensionNew law: 2.67% per year up to 30 years then 4%, with a minimum of AED 10,000. Old law: 60% for 15 years then 2% a year.Decree-Law 57/2023
Net salary after pension11% for nationals registered since October 2023 and 5% for earlier members, 15% from the employer (2.5% of it from the government in the private sector below 20,000), and nothing for expats.Decree-Law 57/2023
Salary date countdownFederal salaries on the 30th, moved earlier for holidays; GPSSA pensions by schedule (26 November and 25 December 2026); the Abu Dhabi fund on the 26th; and private-sector pay due on the 1st since June 2026.MoF circular 2019 and Resolution 0340/2026
Family visa costEntry, residence, Emirates ID and medical in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and the AED 4,000 (or 3,000 with housing) salary rule.ICP and GDRFA Dubai fees
Golden and Green VisasGolden, 10 years: AED 30,000 salary at skill level 1 or 2, or AED 2 million in property or investment. Green, 5 years: AED 15,000 or AED 360,000 freelance income. Retirees: 55 with AED 1 million or AED 240,000 income.Cabinet Resolution 65/2022
Emiratisation targetCompanies with 50 or more workers: 10% of skilled jobs by the end of 2026 (1% every six months), and AED 10,000 a month per missing Emirati. Companies with 20–49 workers in 14 activities: two Emiratis.Ministerial Resolutions 279/2022 and 455/2023
Nafis support for EmiratisFrom September 2026: AED 6,000 for a bachelor's, 5,000 for a diploma and 4,000 for high school on salaries of 6,000–20,000, 600 per child with no limit, and a 500 step-down every 6 months for existing beneficiaries.Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council decisions, 2026
Abu Dhabi pension3.2% per year up to 25 years (80%), then 2% up to 100%, with a minimum of AED 10,200. Early retirement for existing members: 25 years and age 46, then 46.5 from 1 December 2026.Abu Dhabi Law 18/2023
Work injuryFull pay for 6 months then half for 6 months during treatment; 24 months' basic wage between AED 18,000 and 200,000 for death or total disability, and a percentage of it for partial disability.Articles 37 and 38
Dubai rent increaseNo increase if your rent is 10% or less below the index average, then 5% to 20%, with 90 days' notice.Dubai Decree 43/2013
DEWA billElectricity 23–38 fils by slab, water AED 7.70–10.12 per m³, fuel surcharge and 5% VAT, sewerage 2 fils a gallon (2.8 from 2027), and a 5% housing fee on rent.DEWA slab tariff
Abu Dhabi and Al Ain billExpats 26.8 fils inside the green band and 30.5 above, nationals 6.7 and 7.5; water AED 7.84–10.41 or 2.09–2.60; 5% VAT.TAQA Distribution 2025 tariff
Etihad WE billExpats 28–43 fils with the surcharge, nationals 7.5 fils and AED 3.30 per m³; 5% VAT.Etihad WE tariff (Sep 2026)
Fuel pricesNovember 2026: Super 98 AED 4.16, Special 95 4.04, E-Plus 91 3.97 and diesel 4.75 a litre, announced at the end of each month.UAE Fuel Price Committee
Salik tollsAED 6 at peak, AED 4 off-peak and on Sundays, free 1–6 AM, plus 5% VAT since June 2026.Salik
Darb tollsAED 4 per peak crossing (7–9 AM and 3–7 PM), free otherwise and on Sundays and holidays, no monthly cap since September 2025.Abu Dhabi Mobility
Corporate tax0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above, with small business relief (revenue up to AED 3 million) until 2029.Decree-Law 47/2022
Federal government salary scaleGrades 1–14 from a total of AED 5,300 to 27,155, single and married housing, AED 600 per child, and an 11% or 5% pension.Cabinet Resolution 23/2012
Payment of duesWithin 14 days of the end of employment.Article 53
Overstay finesA 30 to 180-day grace period after cancellation by category, then AED 50 a day; no grace for visit visas; late Emirates ID AED 20 a day up to 1,000.Decree-Law 29/2021 and ICP pages
Public holidaysEid Al Etihad on 2–3 December; work on a holiday: day's wage + 50% of basic, or a substitute rest day.Article 28 and Resolution 27/2024
School holidays 2026–2027National Day 2–4 Dec, winter break 14 Dec – 3 Jan, spring break 5–11 Apr, year end 2 Jul.MOE academic calendar
