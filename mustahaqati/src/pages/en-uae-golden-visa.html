Main thresholds

Visa and categoryRequirement
Golden — skilled professionalSkill level 1 or 2, a bachelor's degree, AED 30,000 a month and a valid contract
Golden — property investorOne or more properties worth AED 2 million (AED 2 million paid up in Abu Dhabi)
Golden — investorA deposit, fund or company capital of AED 2 million, or AED 250,000 in taxes a year
Golden — entrepreneurAnnual revenue of AED 1 million, a previous project sold for AED 7 million, or approval from an accredited incubator
Golden — students and graduatesHigh school 95%, a UAE university GPA of 3.5 (A) or 3.8 (B), or a top-100 university with 3.5, within two years of graduating
Golden — by nominationScientists, talents in the arts, sport and technology, inventors, humanitarian workers and frontline heroes
Green — skilled employeeSkill level 1 to 3, a bachelor's degree and AED 15,000 a month
Green — freelancerA freelance permit, a bachelor's or specialised diploma, and AED 360,000 income over the previous two years
Green — investor or partnerA contribution of at least AED 1 million
Retirees (5 years)Age 55 or 15 years of service, with AED 1 million in property or deposits, or AED 240,000 a year in income

Family, absence and grace period

  • Golden: sponsor your spouse, children "regardless of age" and parents, and domestic workers according to your means; you are exempt from the 180-day absence rule, and your family can stay after your death until their permits expire.
  • Green: spouse, sons up to 25, unmarried daughters, children of determination and first-degree relatives, with a 180-day grace period after cancellation.
  • Retirees: 5 years, renewable, with health insurance; Dubai's grace period is 60 days.

Examples:
An engineer at skill level 1 with a bachelor's and a basic salary of AED 32,000: Golden Visa as a skilled professional, and the Green Visa too. With a basic of 20,000 and a gross of 32,000, the Golden Visa is possible but needs checking, because Dubai reportedly uses the basic salary.
An accountant at skill level 2 earning AED 18,000: Green Visa as a skilled employee, not Golden.
A 56-year-old retiree with a paid-off property worth AED 1.2 million: retirement visa under the regulations; Dubai's page also asks for 15 years of service.

This is an estimate: the official portal's Golden Visa pages still show old rules (5 years for property, AED 50,000 for managers), so we rely on the regulations and the 2026 pages of the federal authority, Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
