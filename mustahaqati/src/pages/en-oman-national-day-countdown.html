About Oman National Day

Oman's National Day is 20 and 21 November under Royal Decree 15/2025, which amended Royal Decree 88/2022 determining the official holidays. In 2026, 20 November fell on a Friday, and the official holiday was moved to Wednesday 25 and Thursday 26 November in the 2026 holiday dates the Ministry of Labour announced in advance on 28 December 2025.
