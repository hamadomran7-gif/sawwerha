The payment rule

  • The 1st of each Gregorian month since 1 May 2024 for the civil and military retirement systems and GOSI; civil and military pensions used to be paid on the 25th.
  • Friday and Saturday: payment moves to the Thursday before, giving two payments in December 2026 and in April and September 2027.
  • Public holidays: payment moves to the last working day before; it is not brought forward just because Eid is near (June 2026 was paid on time after Eid al-Adha).
  • SANED: paid with pensions under the same rule.

To estimate the pension itself use the GOSI pension calculator, and for other dates the monthly payment calendar.
