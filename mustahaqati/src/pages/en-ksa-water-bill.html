Calculate your bill

From your bill or the NWC app. A typical household uses about 20 to 30 m³.
SAR 20 for water and 10 for sewage are added for each unit after the first.

The tiers are progressive: each cubic metre is charged at its own tier's rate, not at the highest rate. The current tariff has five tiers; the new one has four.

Homes pay SAR 20 for water and 10 for sewage for each additional unit. Government accounts pay fixed fees of SAR 1,000 for water and 500 for sewage, and SAR 10 per m³ for water and 5 for sewage.

Example: a home with a 20 mm meter uses 30 m³ in a month.

Water: 15 × 1 + 15 × 2 = SAR 45. Sewage: half of that, SAR 22.50. Fixed fees: 20 + 10 = SAR 30.
SAR 97.50 before tax, and SAR 112.13 with 15% VAT.
Under the current tariff: 15 × 0.10 + 15 × 1 = SAR 16.50 for water and 8.25 for sewage, SAR 24.75 in all, or SAR 28.46 with VAT (without any current meter fee).

Before you rely on the figure

  • The new figures come from the National Water Company's announcement as reported by the press; your official bill is the reference.
  • The comparison with the current tariff leaves out any meter fee on your current bill, because its amount is not published.
  • Bills that span 2026 and 2027 charge consumption before 1 January 2027 at the current tariff and the rest at the new one.
