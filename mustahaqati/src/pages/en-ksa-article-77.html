Calculate Article 77 compensation

Basic salary plus fixed allowances.

How Article 77 compensation works

  • Open-ended contract: 15 days of the last actual wage for each year of service.
  • Fixed-term contract: the wage for the period left until the contract's end.
  • Minimum: never less than two months' wage.
  • Contract terms: if your contract sets a specific compensation for termination without a valid reason, that applies instead.

Example: actual wage SAR 10,000, open-ended contract, 3 years and 7 months of service. 3.58 years × 15 days × SAR 333.33 ≈ SAR 17,917 — below two months' wage, so the compensation is SAR 20,000.
