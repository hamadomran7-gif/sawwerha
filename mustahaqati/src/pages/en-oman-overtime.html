Work out your overtime pay

Basic only: the law calculates overtime on it.

Overtime rules in Oman

ItemRule
Normal hours8 a day and 40 a week, with two consecutive rest days (Articles 70 and 77)
Ramadan6 a day or 30 a week for Muslim workers
Daily limit12 hours including overtime, with the worker's consent (Article 71)
Overtime with consentBasic + 25% by day, + 50% at night (9 pm–5 am)
Rest day or holiday100% of the daily basic wage on top of the day's pay, or compensatory leave
Without consent (stocktaking up to 15 days, emergencies)+ 50% by day, + 75% at night, and 200% on rest days and holidays (Article 72)

Example: Basic salary OMR 6,000, so the hourly wage is 6,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = OMR 25. Ten daytime hours: 10 × 25 × 1.25 = 312.500; four rest-day hours paid in cash: 4 × 25 × 2 = 200. Total OMR 512.500.

The law does not set the hourly divisor; ÷ 30 ÷ 8 fits the "daily basic wage" wording, while a 40-hour-week divisor (÷ 173.33) raises the hourly wage by about 38%. The rest-day amount can also be read differently (the premium alone, or a flat daily sum), so treat it as an estimate.
