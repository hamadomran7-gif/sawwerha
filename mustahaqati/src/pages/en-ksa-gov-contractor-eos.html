Calculate your award

Without the housing and transport allowances.

The award in the standard contract for non-Saudis

SituationEntitlement
Contract end, resignation or death from non-work causesHalf a month's salary for each full year, after 3 consecutive years, up to SAR 50,000
Job abolished or dismissal in the public interestTwo months' salary or the remaining contract, whichever is less
Death or permanent disability caused by workTreated as a Saudi employee
Disciplinary dismissal and Article 19 casesNo award or compensation

Leave, tickets and allowances in the contract

ItemRule
Annual leave36 days on full pay for each year, pro rata for part years, paid in advance
TicketsEconomy for the employee and family up to 4 people: at the start and end, and one return trip a year, or half their value
Housing3 months' salary a year in advance, at least SAR 8,000, unless housing is provided
TransportSAR 300, 350 or 400 depending on salary
Sick leaveOne month on full pay and two on half pay for each contract year

Examples with a salary of SAR 10,000:
Resignation after 7 full continuous years: 0.5 × 10,000 × 7 = SAR 35,000 (under the Labour Law it would be reduced for resignation to 30,000).
Contract end after 5 years and 8 months: 5 full years = SAR 25,000.
Resignation after 2 years and 11 months: under 3 continuous years = no award.

These are the terms of the standard contract attached to the Civil Service HR Implementing Regulation (1440H). Universities and bodies with their own regulations use their own contracts, and anyone employed by a government body under the Labour Law should use the private-sector end-of-service calculator. It is not settled whether the cost-of-living allowance counts as salary, or whether unused leave is paid at the end of the contract.
