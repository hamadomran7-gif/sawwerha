The age falls by 4 months for each additional year of age between these rows. The early-retirement period for affected members is 360 months for those with under 180 months that day, falling by 12 months for each further 12 months, down to 300 months for those with 228 to 239 months.

Examples:
Born 10 May 1990, contributing since 1 January 2012: aged 34 on 3 July 2024 with 150 months, so affected; retirement age 63, on 10 May 2053, and early retirement after 360 months.
Born 1 March 1975, contributing since 2000: aged 49 that day, so not affected; retires at 60 Hijri years in 2033, and completed the 300-month early-retirement period in early 2025.
Born in 2000, first insured in 2025: retirement age 65, in 2065, with reduced early retirement from 2055.
