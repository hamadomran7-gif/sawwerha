Calculate the heirs' shares

Capped at one-third of what remains after debts.
More heirs: grandchildren, grandparents, half-siblings and uncles

Order of claims on the estate

Under Article 198 of the Personal Status Law: funeral costs in a customary way, then the deceased's debts (secured debts first), then the bequest up to one-third, then division of the rest among the heirs. A bequest above one-third or to an heir depends on the heirs' consent, and a bequest above one-third is valid for someone with no heirs (Arts. 179, 190 and 191).

What is specific to Saudi law

  • The grandfather excludes siblings: he inherits like the father and excludes the siblings (Art. 212), so there is no sharing and no Akdariyya.
  • No sharing in the Mushtaraka: the residuary drops out when the fixed shares use up the estate (Art. 227).
  • The 'Umariyya cases: the mother takes one-third of the remainder when the heirs are the parents and one spouse (Art. 213).
  • The father does not exclude his mother: the paternal grandmother takes 1/6 with the father, and grandmothers are excluded by the mother (Art. 214).
  • Reduction and return: shares are reduced proportionally when they exceed the estate (Art. 230), and any remainder returns to heirs other than spouses in proportion to their shares, reaching a spouse only after distant kin (Art. 231).
  • No obligatory bequest: the children of a son who died before his father get no automatic share alongside a living son.

What the calculator does not cover

An unborn child, a missing person, an intersex heir, several people dying together, the distribution among distant kin, and grandparents or grandchildren beyond the first degree. A person who killed the deceased intentionally does not inherit, and there is no inheritance across religions (Arts. 200 and 202), so do not enter them. The calculator is for illustration and does not replace the court's heirship deed and division.

Examples:
Wife, son and daughter: wife 1/8, son 7/12, daughter 7/24; an estate of 300,000 gives 37,500, 175,000 and 87,500.
Husband, mother and father ('Umariyya): husband 1/2, mother one-third of the remainder (1/6), father 1/3.
Husband, two full sisters and mother: the case is reduced to 8 parts; husband 3/8, each sister 1/4, mother 1/8.
Mother, grandfather, full brother and full sister: mother 1/6, grandfather the remaining 5/6, the siblings excluded by the grandfather.
