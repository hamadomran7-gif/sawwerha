Work out the benefit

The 3 months before the injury, capped at 45,000.

Occupational hazards benefits

CaseSaudiNon-Saudi
TreatmentDaily allowance of 100% of the daily contributory wage, 75% while treated at GOSI's expense, until recovery or disability is established
Total disabilityPension of 100% of the average (minimum 1,983.75 or 2,000), + 50% for constant help84 times the pension in one payment
Disability of 50% or moreMonthly pension = degree × average60 times the partial pension
Disability under 50%Degree × average × 60, falling by one for each year over 45, minimum 36 times, no cap
DeathSurvivors' pension of 50% or 75% (or 100% for three under the old system), plus a 3-month grant up to 10,000 under the old system84 times the pension

Examples on a wage of SAR 8,000:
Temporary disability of 45 days: 45 × 266.67 = SAR 12,000, or 11,333.33 with 10 days in hospital at GOSI's expense.
30% disability at age 35: 2,400 × 60 = SAR 144,000; at age 52: 2,400 × 53 = 127,200.
60% disability: a pension of SAR 4,800 a month for a Saudi, and 60 × 4,800 = 288,000 for a non-Saudi.
Death of a non-Saudi: 84 × 8,000 = SAR 672,000.

The worker must be registered with GOSI before the injury, and the employer must report it within 3 days of being told. Daily allowances must be claimed within a year and other benefits within 5 years. This is an estimate: the disability degree is set by the medical committees, and the official amount is in GOSI's decision. For ordinary sick leave, see the sick leave calculator. Prefer Arabic?
