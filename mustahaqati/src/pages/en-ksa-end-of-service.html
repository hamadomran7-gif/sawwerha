Calculate your end-of-service award

Basic salary plus fixed allowances (housing, transport…): the "actual wage" in the law.

How the Saudi end-of-service award is calculated

ServiceFull award (Article 84)On resignation (Article 85)
Less than 2 yearsHalf a month per yearNothing
2 to 5 yearsHalf a month per yearOne third
More than 5, under 10 yearsHalf a month for each of the first 5, then a full monthTwo thirds
10 years or moreHalf a month for each of the first 5, then a full monthFull award

Example: last wage SAR 9,000 and 8 years of service. First five years: 5 × 4,500 = 22,500. Three more years: 3 × 9,000 = 27,000. Full award: SAR 49,500. On resignation (more than five years): two thirds = SAR 33,000.

The award is calculated on the last actual wage: basic salary plus fixed allowances such as housing and transport (Article 2 of the Labour Law). Part years count pro rata.

Exceptions: full award despite resigning (Article 87)

  • Leaving work because of force majeure beyond the worker's control.
  • A female worker ending her contract within six months of marriage or three months of giving birth.

When is the award lost?

If the employer terminates the contract for one of the Article 80 cases, such as assaulting the employer or a manager, a serious breach of obligations, or unexcused absence of more than 30 separate days or 15 consecutive days in a year after written warning. The worker may challenge this before the labour court, and the employer must prove the reason. See Article 80, and for leaving without notice because of the employer's breach, Article 81, which keeps the full award.

Saudi Arabia vs. the UAE

In the UAE the gratuity is calculated on basic salary, in days (21, then 30 a year), and is not reduced on resignation. In Saudi Arabia it is calculated on the actual wage, in months (half, then a full month), and is cut to a third or two thirds on resignation before ten years.
