Calculate your leave

Do not include transport allowance or expenses.

Leave rules in Kuwait's private sector

  • Annual leave: at least 30 working days a year; weekly rest days, public holidays and sick days do not count against it.
  • First year: due after six months of service, pro rata for part years.
  • Pay: the last full wage — basic plus regular allowances.
  • Sick leave: 15 days full pay, 10 at three-quarters, 10 at half, 10 at a quarter, then 30 days unpaid.

Example: last full wage KWD 1,000 and 20 unused working days: 1,000 ÷ 26 × 20 = KWD 769.23, or KWD 666.67 dividing by 30.
