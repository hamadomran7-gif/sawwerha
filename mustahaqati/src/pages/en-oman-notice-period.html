Work out your notice and pay in lieu

Pay in lieu uses the last total wage (Article 38).

Notice rules in Oman

ItemRule
Notice period30 days for monthly-paid workers and 15 for others, unless the contract sets more (Article 38)
ConditionWritten notice and a legitimate reason
Pay in lieuThe last total wage for the period or the part not served
Job searchUp to 10 paid hours a week in all cases (Article 39)
Non-renewal of a fixed-term contractThe employer must give at least one month's notice
ProbationUp to 3 months for monthly pay and 2 for others, ended with 7 days' notice (Article 37)

Example: A total wage of OMR 8,000 a month: 30 days' notice. If the employer ends the contract immediately, pay in lieu is OMR 8,000; a worker who leaves after 10 days owes 20 × 8,000 ÷ 30 = OMR 5,333.333.
