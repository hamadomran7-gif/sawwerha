What goes into employee cost in Saudi Arabia

ItemSaudiExpat
Employer GOSI share11.75% (9% pension + 2% hazards + 0.75% SANED), slightly higher for new registrants2% occupational hazards
GOSI wage baseBasic plus housing, capped at SAR 45,000 a month
Expat levy—SAR 800 or 700 a month
Work permit and iqama—SAR 100 + 650 (or 500) a year
End-of-service provisionHalf a month per year for the first five years, then a month

Example: an expat on SAR 6,000 basic, 1,500 housing and 500 other allowances, more expats than Saudis, SAR 2,500 medical insurance, in the first year: salary 8,000, hazards 150, levy 800, permit and iqama 62.50, insurance 208.33 and end-of-service provision 333.33, about SAR 9,554 a month, or 19% above salary.
