Work out your notice and pay in lieu

Pay in lieu uses the last basic wage and social allowance (Article 47).

Notice rules in Bahrain

ItemRule
Notice period30 days for either side, whatever the wage or service (Article 99/a)
Longer noticeBy agreement, only where the employer terminates
Pay in lieuThe last basic wage plus social allowance for the period or the part not served (Articles 99/b and 47)
If the employer does not observe itThe notice, or what is left of it, counts as service
Job searchIf the employer gives notice: a day or 8 hours a week on full pay (Article 99/c)
ProbationUp to 3 months (6 for listed jobs), ended with one day's notice (Article 21)

Example: A basic wage of BHD 6,000 with no social allowance: if the employer ends the contract without notice, pay in lieu is BHD 6,000 and the 30 days count as service; a worker who walks out immediately owes the same, and the contract ends that day.
