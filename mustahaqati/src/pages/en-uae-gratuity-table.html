Three things the table shows

  1. Year six is worth more than any year before it: it adds a full month's basic salary, while each of the first five years adds 0.7 of a month.
  2. The cap starts at 25.5 years: gratuity cannot exceed 24 months' pay, so it stops rising after 25.5 years however long you work.
  3. Only basic salary counts: an employee on a basic of 8,000 and a total of 15,000 gets the 8,000 column, not the 15,000 one.

The formula used

Daily wage = basic salary ÷ 30. Gratuity = (first 5 years × 21 days + later years × 30 days) × daily wage, capped at 24 × basic salary, provided you complete one year of continuous service. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
