Three things the table shows

  1. The increase is 3 halalas per kWh in both tiers, or SAR 34.50 per 1,000 kWh including VAT.
  2. The fixed fee adds SAR 80.50 including VAT for a breaker under 100 A, replacing the current meter fees.
  3. Consumption above 6,000 kWh is charged at 33 halalas, so cutting it saves the most.
