Article 113 leave runs from the date of the event itself in calendar days, and the employer may ask for supporting documents. A mother's 12-week maternity leave is worked out in the maternity leave calculator.

Examples:
An employee's father dies on 1 November: the leave is 5 days, 1 to 5 November, and he returns on 6 November.
A child is born on 1 November and the father starts his leave on 5 November: it ends on 7 November, the last day of the 7-day window.

How is a widow's iddah counted?

The law sets the period at four months and ten days from the date of death without naming the calendar. In Islamic law the iddah is counted in lunar months, which gives about 126 to 130 days; in Gregorian months it is about 130 to 133 days.
