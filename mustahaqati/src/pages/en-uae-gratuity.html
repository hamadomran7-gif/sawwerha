Calculate your gratuity

Deducted from your service, as the law requires.

How UAE gratuity is calculated

  1. Daily wage = monthly basic salary ÷ 30.
  2. First five years: 21 days' wage for each year of service.
  3. After five years: 30 days' wage for each additional year.
  4. Part years are paid pro rata once you complete one year.
  5. Cap: the total may not exceed two years' basic salary.

Example: basic salary AED 8,000 and 7 years of service. Daily wage 266.67. First five years: 5 × 21 = 105 days = AED 28,000. Two more years: 2 × 30 = 60 days = AED 16,000. Total gratuity: AED 44,000.

Resignation and termination

Under the current law the gratuity is the same whether you resign or the employer ends the contract, as long as you have completed one year of continuous service. It is paid within 14 days of the contract ending. Employees dismissed for one of the gross-misconduct cases in Article 44 keep their gratuity under the current law; see Article 44 dismissal and gratuity after resignation.

What changed from the old law?

Under the old Federal Law 8 of 1980, an employee who resigned before five years lost part of the gratuity. The current Decree-Law 33 of 2021 removed that reduction: resignation, termination by the employer and the end of the contract are all treated the same once you have completed a year of service. You can still lose part of it in two ways: a court ruling in specific cases, or a deduction to settle a debt you owe the employer.

What is not included

  • Allowances (housing, transport, phone, commission): only the basic salary counts.
  • Unpaid absence days are deducted from your service; see how unpaid leave reduces gratuity.
  • Service before 2 February 2022 is calculated under the current law too, because the law in force when the contract ends applies to the whole period.

Special cases

  • Part-time and flexible work: the gratuity is prorated by your actual working hours compared with full-time hours, under the executive regulations.
  • Emiratis are covered by the pension system instead; see the UAE pension calculator.
  • Domestic workers have their own rule; see the domestic worker gratuity calculator.
  • Alternative savings scheme: an employer may join a voluntary savings scheme in which the gratuity is invested through licensed funds; you then receive the fund balance instead of the gratuity for that period.
  • Death: the gratuity is paid to the employee's heirs.

What else is in your final settlement?

Besides the gratuity, the employer must pay your remaining salary, cash for unused annual leave and, if notice was not served, pay in lieu of notice. Add them up with the final settlement calculator, the annual leave calculator and the notice pay calculator. If payment is late or disputed, you can file a labour complaint through the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation app or website; unresolved cases go to court.
