Calculate your gratuity

Basic salary only, without allowances, as written in your contract.
Deducted from your service, as the law requires.

How UAE gratuity is calculated

  1. Daily wage = monthly basic salary ÷ 30.
  2. First five years: 21 days' wage for each year of service.
  3. After five years: 30 days' wage for each additional year.
  4. Part years are paid pro rata once you complete one year.
  5. Cap: the total may not exceed two years' basic salary.

Example: basic salary AED 8,000 and 7 years of service. Daily wage 266.67. First five years: 5 × 21 = 105 days = AED 28,000. Two more years: 2 × 30 = 60 days = AED 16,000. Total gratuity: AED 44,000.

Resignation and termination

Under the current law the gratuity is the same whether you resign or the employer ends the contract, as long as you have completed one year of continuous service. It is paid within 14 days of the contract ending. Employees dismissed for one of the gross-misconduct cases in Article 44 keep their gratuity under the current law.
