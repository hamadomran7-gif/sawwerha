Calculate your leave balance

UAE annual leave rules

  • Entitlement: 30 days for each year of service, pro rata for part years.
  • First year: nothing before six months; 2 days for each month between six months and a year.
  • Cash in lieu: at the end of service, unused days are paid at basic salary ÷ 30.

Example: basic salary AED 7,000, started 1 February 2024, 40 days taken by 30 November 2026. Accrued 85 days; remaining 45 days, worth AED 10,500 if paid out.
