Count your Saudi employees

One wage per line or separated by commas. Write “4500x10” for ten employees on the same wage. For one employee, enter just their wage.

Special categories (Decision 61706)

CategoryWeightConditions
Part-time½Wage of SAR 3,000 or more registered with GOSI, with no more than two employers

Student working part-time½Wage of SAR 3,000, up to 10% of Saudi staff (40% for restaurants and cafés)
Person with a disability able to work4Wage of SAR 4,000 or more, up to 10% of Saudi staff; any above that count as one
Released prisoner2For two years after release, on SAR 4,000, up to 10%

All special categories together may not exceed 15% of the establishment's Saudi staff. The 2027 and 2028 increases cover five categories — full-time, part-time, flexible work, part-time students and persons with disabilities — and the new thresholds for the special categories have not been announced yet.

Example: an establishment has five Saudis on SAR 3,500, 4,200, 4,800, 5,500 and 6,500.

Today: four count fully and one as half = 4.5.
From 25 April 2027: two fully and two as half = 3; raising three wages by SAR 2,500 a month in total would make everyone count fully.
From 25 April 2028: one fully and one as half = 1.5.
