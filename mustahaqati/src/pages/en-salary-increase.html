Work out your raise

To see the real change in your purchasing power.

How a pay rise is calculated

  • Raise percentage = (new salary − old salary) ÷ old salary × 100.
  • Salary after a raise = salary × (1 + percentage ÷ 100).
  • After years of a fixed raise = salary × (1 + percentage ÷ 100) to the power of the number of years, because each raise applies to the salary after the previous one.

Examples:
From 8,000 to 8,800: 800 more a month (9,600 a year), a raise of 10%.
A salary of 10,000 with a 4% raise every year for 5 years becomes 12,166.53 — more than 20%, even though 5 × 4% = 20%.

How a raise affects your entitlements

End-of-service gratuity is calculated on your last wage, so each raise increases it for all your years of service. Check the difference in the Saudi end-of-service calculator or the UAE gratuity calculator. In Saudi Arabia, the GOSI contribution deducted from your wage also rises; see the net salary calculator.
