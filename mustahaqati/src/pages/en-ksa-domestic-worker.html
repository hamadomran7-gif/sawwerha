Calculate a domestic worker's dues

30 days for every two years if the contract was renewed.
Two months unless the contract says otherwise.

Domestic worker rights under the 2023 regulation

RightEntitlementArticle
End of serviceA month's wage for every 4 consecutive years, on the last wage22
Unlawful terminationTwo months' wage unless the contract says otherwise25
Annual leave30 paid days after two years on renewal, and a ticket every two years12
Sick leave30 days: 15 on full pay, then 15 on half pay13
Hours and restAt most 10 working hours a day, 8 hours' continuous rest, and a paid 24-hour weekly rest10, 11
Final duesWithin a week if the employer ends the contract, two weeks if the worker does23

Example: wage SAR 2,000 and 9 years of service ending when the contract expired: two months' wage = SAR 4,000 (SAR 4,500 pro rata).
