5% VAT is added to every crossing from 1 June 2026. Sunday rates do not apply when the Sunday is a public holiday or a public event day.

The ten Salik gates

Al Barsha, Al Garhoud Bridge, Business Bay Crossing, Al Maktoum Bridge, Al Mamzar South, Al Mamzar North, Al Safa South, Al Safa North, Airport Tunnel and Jebel Ali.

Example: an employee crossing two gates at peak time every day, five days a week: 2 × AED 6.30 = AED 12.60 a day, AED 63 a week and about AED 273 a month.
