Your own monthly income is deducted from the allowance, with a minimum payment of SAR 100, and payment stops if your income reaches the allowance due. Household income is only an eligibility test.

Examples:
A graduate with no income: SAR 20,250 over 15 months.
Own income of SAR 600: 1,400, then 900, then 400, then 150, for a total of SAR 11,250.
Own income of SAR 800: 1,200, then 700, then 200, then it stops in the last tier, for a total of SAR 8,400.

Anyone who has just lost a job should look at SANED unemployment benefit, because Hafiz only accepts people who worked before 24 months after their job ended. The allowance comes with obligations: logging in weekly, attending training and interviews, and accepting suitable work; refusing two suitable offers stops payment.
