The reduction applies to the basic pension only, and there is none for men with 35 years of service or women with 30. Service beyond 30 years is paid as a lump sum using the retirement lump-sum bands.

Examples:
A man aged 55 with 25 years of service and a salary of KD 2,000 (KD 1,500 counts): 85% = KD 1,275, plus a remuneration of 18 × 1,500 = KD 27,000.
A man aged 52 with 26 years 6 months of service and a pension salary of KD 1,400: 88% = KD 1,232, and after a 15% permanent reduction KD 1,047.20, raised to KD 1,126 for a married man if the minimum applies to the reduced pension.

If you do not qualify for a pension

Anyone whose service ends before 15 years receives a retirement lump sum: pension salary × 12 × 10% for each of the first 5 years, then 12% for the next five, then 15%, then 20% for the rest. Twelve years on a pension salary of KD 1,200 gives KD 20,160. For expatriates in Kuwait's private sector, see the end-of-service indemnity calculator.
