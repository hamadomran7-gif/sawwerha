Legal working hour limits

RuleSaudi ArabiaUAE
Normal hours8 a day or 48 a week8 a day or 48 a week
Ramadan6 a day and 36 a week for MuslimsTwo hours less a day
BreakAt least half an hour after 5 consecutive hours, not counted as workAt least one hour after 5 consecutive hours
Daily attendance12 hours at most at the workplace—
Overtime limit720 hours a year unless the worker agrees to moreTwo hours a day, and 144 working hours every 3 weeks
Overtime payActual wage + 50% of basicBasic + 25% (50% at night)

Examples:
8 am to 6 pm with a one-hour break, 5 days: 9 hours a day and 45 a week. On the daily standard that is 5 overtime hours a week; on the weekly standard there is no overtime because 45 is under 48.
In Ramadan in Saudi Arabia, 7 actual hours on 5 days is 35 hours, under 36, but each day is one hour over 6 on the daily standard.
