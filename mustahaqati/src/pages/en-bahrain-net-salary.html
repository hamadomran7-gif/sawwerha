Examples (2026):
Bahraini, basic 1,000 and allowances 400: 8% × 1,400 = 112 is deducted and net pay is BHD 1,288; the employer pays 18% = 252.
Bahraini, basic 1,000 and allowances 1,500: allowances count at 1,000 only (100% of basic), so insurable pay is 2,000 and 160 is deducted.
Expat on 1,400 in the first 3 years: 14 is deducted and net pay is 1,386; the employer pays 3% + 4.2% = 100.8.

Insurable pay for Bahrainis is basic plus fixed allowances paid to all workers in the same category (cash housing, transport, social and others) up to 100% of basic, capped at BHD 4,000. Decree-Law 44/2026 adds a minimum insurable wage for first-time registrants after 9 October 2026; the amount has not been set yet. In government, contributions are on basic salary: 7% from the employee and 20% from the employer.
