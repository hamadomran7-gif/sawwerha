Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service gratuityA month's basic salary for each year since 31 July 2023; earlier service under the old law (15 days for the first 3 years, then a month).Article 61
Annual leaveAt least 30 days on the full wage after 6 months, with unused days paid on the full wage when service ends.Articles 78 and 81
Sick leaveUp to 182 days a year: 21 on full pay, 14 at 75%, 35 at 50%, then 112 at 35%.Article 82
Maternity leave98 days on total pay reimbursed by the Social Protection Fund, 7 days for fathers within 98 days, and a nursing hour for a year.Labour Law Arts. 76 and 84; Social Protection Law
Personal income tax (from 2028)5% on gross annual income above OMR 42,000, after rental and self-employment costs and exemptions for pension contributions, education, healthcare and donations.Royal Decree 56/2025
Pension (Omanis)2% per year of service on revalued wages, unreduced at 60 for men and 55 for women, early retirement reduced 5% a year, and no refund of contributions.Social Protection Law 52/2023
Job security allowanceFor Omanis whose job ended for reasons beyond their control: 60% of the two-year average wage for 6 months, then 50% for three and 40% for three; minimum OMR 115.Social Protection Law 52/2023
OvertimeBasic + 25% by day and + 50% at night; rest day 100% extra or leave; without consent + 50%, + 75% and 200%.Articles 70–73
Notice period30 days for monthly pay and 15 otherwise; pay in lieu on the last total wage; 10 hours a week to look for work.Articles 38 and 39
Public holidaysNational Day on 25–26 November 2026; work on a holiday: day's wage + 100% of basic, or compensatory leave.Articles 71 and 79, Decree 88/2022
