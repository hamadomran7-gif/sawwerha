How to work out your fuel cost

Full tank = tank size × price per litre. Monthly cost = (monthly distance × litres per 100 km ÷ 100) × price per litre. A car driving 2,000 km a month at 8 litres per 100 km needs 160 litres, or AED 646.40 with Special 95 in November 2026.
