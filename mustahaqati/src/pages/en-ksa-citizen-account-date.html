The deposit rule

  • The 10th of each Gregorian month, the same rule since 2017.
  • Friday: the deposit moves to Thursday the 9th.
  • Saturday: the deposit moves to Sunday the 11th.
  • Eid: no written rule, but the April 2024 deposit came a week early because it fell on the first day of Eid al-Fitr; the next case is March 2027.

The extra support has been extended to 31 December 2026 and amounts may change in 2027; the deposit day is not affected. For salaries, social security and Hafiz see the monthly payment calendar.
