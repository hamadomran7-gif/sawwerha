Pay for working on a holiday

If you have to work on a public holiday, your employer must give you another rest day for each day, or pay the day's wage plus at least 50% of your basic wage (Article 28(2)). We calculate the day's wage on total pay ÷ 30 and the premium on basic ÷ 30, on top of your monthly salary, which already covers the holiday itself.

Example: basic AED 6,000 and total AED 8,000, working one Eid day: 8,000 ÷ 30 = 266.67, and 50% × 6,000 ÷ 30 = 100, so you are owed AED 366.67 or a substitute rest day.

Holidays that fall on the weekend

A holiday that falls on the weekend or on another holiday is not carried over (Article 3 of Resolution 27/2024). The Cabinet can move holidays other than the Eids to the start or end of the week, as it moved the Prophet's Birthday in 2026 to Friday 28 August. A holiday inside your annual leave counts as part of it unless your contract is more generous (Article 29).

Government sector

The list has been the same for both sectors since 2019, and emirate governments may add holidays for their own bodies. Commemoration Day (1 December) has not been a paid holiday since 2025.

Hijri dates are expected dates based on the Umm al-Qura calendar and may move by a day depending on the moon sighting.
