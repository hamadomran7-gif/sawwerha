What a final settlement in Bahrain includes

ItemHow it is calculatedArticle
End-of-service gratuityHalf a month per year for the first 3 years then a month, on basic plus social allowance; service since 1/3/2024 is paid by the SIO116 and Decision 109/2023
Leave pay(Basic + social allowance) ÷ 30 × unused days58 and 59
Notice30 days, on basic plus social allowance99
Unpaid salaryFull wage ÷ 30 × unpaid days—

Example: Basic 500, social allowance 100 and allowances 100, 6 years and 10 months, and 10 leave days: gratuity 3,200 (1,600 from the employer and 1,600 from the SIO) and leave pay 200, a total of BHD 3,400, of which 1,800 from the employer.

Notice pay covers the unserved days: the employer pays it when ending the contract without full notice, and it is deducted from a worker who leaves without it. For detail use the end-of-service calculator and the leave calculator.
