Count the period between two dates

When you need this calculator

  • Notice period: have 30 or 60 days passed since you handed in notice?
  • Leave: how many working days does leave from one date to another use?
  • Probation: when do the six months or 90 days end?
  • Residence permits and contracts: how long is left until the expiry date?

Example: from Sunday 1 November to Tuesday 1 December 2026 is 30 days, one month. With a Friday–Saturday weekend, 22 of them are working days.

For the length of service your gratuity is based on, use your country's calculator, such as UAE gratuity or Saudi end of service, because they apply the rules for part years. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
