Calculate your health scale salary

It varies by category and level; choose the amount on your payslip.

How the health jobs scale works

The current health jobs salary scale was issued by Royal Order 4097/M B on 25/6/1432H, and we found no newer scale up to November 2026. It has 11 categories, each with 7 levels of 4 steps and a fixed increment. The scale is continuous: the first step of the next level equals step 4 plus the level's increment. Physicians and pharmacists receive a full-time allowance, and the others a 20% working-hours allowance, calculated on the level's first step, not your step.

Health practitioner allowances

  • Full-time allowance: physicians 70% at level 1, falling 5 points per level to 40% at level 7; pharmacists 45% down to 20%. Others receive a 20% working-hours allowance. All on the level's first step.
  • Infection: SAR 750 a month for staff directly exposed in isolation, quarantine, TB and similar units.
  • Danger: SAR 600 a month for jobs exposed to danger.
  • 90% cap: the full-time, forensic, isolation, psychiatric and danger allowances together may not exceed 90% of the level's first step; transport is outside the cap.
  • Supervision: SAR 3,000 in general hospitals and central labs, and SAR 5,000 in specialist and referral hospitals, for medical directors and heads of department.
  • Scarcity and excellence: up to 30% of the level's first step subject to conditions and committees, and 10% for a Saudi resident physician in small cities. Excellence requires an "excellent" rating in the last two appraisals.
  • Remote areas: up to 20%, mountain areas 10% to 50%, not combined with other location allowances.
  • Transport: SAR 500, 700, 900 or 1,200 depending on category and level.

Allowances not included in the calculator (scarcity, excellence, remote areas, training, psychiatric and forensic) are granted by individual decisions, so add them from your payslip.

Increments and promotion

The annual increment follows the Civil Service Law, so it is paid on 1 January and moves you one step within the level. After step 4 you move to the next level after at least a year at the top of the level and two latest ratings of very good or above. A consultant physician at the end of level 7 receives an amount equal to the level's increment each year as a bonus that does not change the basic.

The SAR 500 allowance from January 2027

The royal order of 25 October 2026 granted a monthly SAR 500 allowance to civilian and military employees on civil service scales and operating items and to retirees, alongside the electricity and water tariff changes. Newspapers reported that it starts on 1 January 2027 and is not added to basic salary, so no pension is deducted from it and percentage allowances are not calculated on it.

Staff moved to Health Holding

Staff who moved to the health clusters of the Health Holding Company left this scale for the company's pay structure, on condition that their pay did not fall. They received a transfer bonus of 16% of basic salary per year of service, up to four salaries, and moved to social insurance with their service combined.

Examples:
Specialist, level 1 step 1: 7,130 + working-hours allowance 1,426 + transport 700 = SAR 9,256, with a 641.70 deduction.
Resident physician, level 1 step 2: 9,695 + full-time 6,440 + transport 700 = SAR 16,835, with an 872.55 deduction.
Consultant physician, level 1 step 1: 14,950 + full-time 10,465 = SAR 25,415 before transport.

For staff on civil service grades see the general salary scale, for teachers the teachers' scale, and before retiring estimate your pension.
