When you need Hijri dates in the Gulf

  • Saudi iqama and visas: expiry dates and fee periods are often shown in Hijri.
  • Government retirement: the Saudi civil service retirement age is 60 Hijri years, about 58 Gregorian years and 2.5 months.
  • Contracts and leases: some still carry Hijri dates alongside Gregorian ones.
  • Ramadan working hours: reduced hours follow the Hijri month.
