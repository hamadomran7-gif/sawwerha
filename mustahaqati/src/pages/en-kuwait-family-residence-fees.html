Example: a private-sector sponsor, wife and two children, one-year residence: visas 30 plus their health fee 15, residence 60, health insurance 300 and civil IDs 15 = KWD 420, then 375 a year. With a two-year residence the renewal is 735, or 367.5 a year. Parents add KWD 810 a year.

A salary of at least KWD 800 is required, with 9 professions exempt. The civil ID fee may reach KWD 7 with the smart card fee. We found no change announced for 2027. If a residence lapses, work out the fine with the Kuwait overstay fine calculator.
