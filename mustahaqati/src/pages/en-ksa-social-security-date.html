The deposit rule

  • The 1st of each Gregorian month into the breadwinner's bank account.
  • On a weekend or public holiday: another date is set and announced, according to the ministry.
  • Eligibility results: reviewed monthly and published between the 25th and 27th.

To estimate the amount use the social security calculator, and for other dates the monthly payment calendar.
