Work out your family residence cost

Family residence fees in Qatar

ItemAmount (QAR)When
Family residence entry visa200First time
Family member's residence permit400 a year (500 in some sources)Each issue and renewal, 1–3 years, 20% off for three
Qatar ID card100Each issue and renewal
Medical test100At issue, for adults
Health insuranceInsurer's priceCondition of residence

Example: a wife and two children, one-year residence: visas 600, permits 1,200, IDs 300 and medical 100 = QAR 2,200 plus insurance, then 1,500 a year. Renewed for three years: 3,180, or 1,060 a year.

A salary of QAR 10,000, or 6,000 with family housing from the employer, is required, with a technical or specialised job in the private sector; in government, family housing or the salary is enough. Renewing more than 90 days late risks a fine of QAR 10 a day up to 6,000. Family visits cost differently: QAR 200 per 30 days plus QAR 50 a month of insurance.
