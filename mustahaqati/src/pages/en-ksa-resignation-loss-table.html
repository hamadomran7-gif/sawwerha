The short version in four numbers (wage SAR 10,000)

  • At 4 years you lose two thirds: the full award is SAR 20,000 and the resignation award SAR 6,667.
  • From 5 years to 5 years and a month: the resignation award rises from SAR 8,333 to 17,222 — more than double for one month's work.
  • At 9 years you lose a third: SAR 43,333 instead of 65,000.
  • From 9 years 11 months to 10 years: it rises from SAR 49,444 to 75,000.

“Difference” is what a resigning employee loses compared with the contract ending or the employer terminating it. Figures are rounded to the nearest riyal and assume whole years and a constant wage.

Method

The full award follows Article 84: half a month's wage for each of the first five years and a month's wage for each year after, on the last actual wage. The resignation award follows Article 85: nothing before two years, one third up to five years, two thirds above five and below ten, and the full award from ten years. The tables are generated from the same formula as the Saudi end-of-service calculator, so they update with it.

Before you hand in your resignation

  • Calculate your award in days with the resignation calculator, because the thresholds are measured by actual service, not calendar years.
  • Check whether your case is one of the Article 87 exceptions or an Article 81 case that keeps your full award.
  • Don't forget payment for unused annual leave, which is due in every case.
