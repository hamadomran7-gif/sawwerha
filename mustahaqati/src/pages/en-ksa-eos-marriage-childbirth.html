What does Article 87 say?

As an exception to the resignation fractions in Article 85, the full award is paid in two cases that concern female employees:

  • Ending the contract within six months of the date of the marriage contract.
  • Ending the contract within three months of the date of giving birth.

The article also covers any worker who leaves because of force majeure beyond their control.

The example in the calculator above: actual wage SAR 8,000, service of 3 years and 5 months, and the contract ended within six months of marriage.

Full award = 3.417 × 4,000 = SAR 13,667.
An ordinary resignation with the same service would earn only one third: SAR 4,556.

Practical steps

  1. Work out the last allowed date: six months from the marriage contract or three months from the birth.
  2. Give written notice ending the contract before that date, stating that it is under Article 87.
  3. Attach proof of the date (marriage contract or birth certificate) and keep a stamped or electronic copy of your notice.
  4. Check the amount paid against the calculator, and add pay for unused annual leave.
