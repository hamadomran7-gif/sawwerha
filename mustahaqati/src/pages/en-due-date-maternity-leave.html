Work out your due date and leave dates

Usually 28; each extra day moves the due date one day later.

How the due date is calculated

Due date = first day of the last period + 280 days (40 weeks), Naegele's rule as used by the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists. If your cycle is longer than 28 days the difference is added, because ovulation comes later by the same amount. From conception add 266 days; for IVF count from the transfer day: 261 days for a day-5 embryo and 263 for a day-3 embryo.

Pregnancy by weeks

  • First trimester: to the end of week 13, when an ultrasound dates the pregnancy most accurately.
  • Second trimester: weeks 14 to 27.
  • Third trimester: week 28 to birth.
  • Term: early term 37–38, full term 39–40, late term 41, post-term from 42 weeks.

The calculator gives estimates and does not replace your doctor's care. Leave dates assume birth on the due date; an earlier or later birth changes them under each country's law.
