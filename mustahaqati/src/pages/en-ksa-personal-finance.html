How the instalment is calculated

The calculator uses the reducing-balance method at the annual percentage rate, the standard method in bank finance. The rate advertised in offers is usually the APR including fees, and that is the figure to compare.

Example: SAR 100,000 of finance at 7% over 60 months on a salary of SAR 9,000.

Instalment ≈ SAR 1,980. Total cost ≈ SAR 118,800. The deduction ratio is 22%, under the 33% limit, so the finance complies.

Deduction limits

SAMA's instructions require banks to keep total monthly instalments within 33% of an employee's salary and 25% of a retiree's. The aim is to protect customers from default, so all existing obligations count towards the ratio, including the minimum payment on credit cards. For home finance, use the Saudi mortgage calculator.
