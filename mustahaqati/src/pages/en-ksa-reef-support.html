When is Reef paid and how much?

Reef payment dates

The Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture announced that support is paid "from the first day until the tenth" of each Gregorian month for all sectors, that payment frequency differs by sector, and that deposits may arrive at different times depending on the bank. There is no fixed day; saying it is the 1st or the 10th is inaccurate.

How much is the support?

SectorSupportBasis
Productive families10% of capital a month up to 4,500, with the total not above the capitalAjel 2021, Al-Madina 2025
Rain-fed crops2 per kg of grain, 4 for sesame, 0.5 for watermelonReef (2022)
Lemons0.55 per kg and 3.5 per treeReef (2023)
Livestock (50–500 head)8 per sheep or goat, 40 per camel, 60 per cowMinistry (2022)
Small-scale fishermenUp to 54,000 per fishermanMinistry (2024)
All programs54,000 a year cap, up to 3 programsAjel and Al-Madina

Crop and livestock rates come from 2022 and 2023 announcements and may have changed. We found no official rates for beekeeping, coffee or roses, so they are left out. The duration and value of support depend on each sector's cap.

Conditions

  • Saudi or a holder of a movement card, aged 21 or older (18 for fishermen).
  • Not employed in the public or private sector, practising the activity personally and living in the project's administrative region.
  • Monthly income no more than SAR 6,000, or 9,000 for retirement pensions as reported in 2021.
  • An active bank account in their name, proof of the occupation, health and technical requirements, and a freelance document for productive families.

On 20 October 2026 the Council of Ministers extended the program to the end of 2032; beneficiaries exceed 90,000, about 65,000 of them women.

Examples:
A productive family with capital of 30,000: SAR 3,000 a month for 10 months.
A family with capital of 60,000: 4,500 a month (the maximum), 54,000 in the first year.
A rain-fed farmer producing 2,000 kg of wheat and 3,000 kg of watermelon: 4,000 + 1,500 = SAR 5,500 at 2022 rates.
