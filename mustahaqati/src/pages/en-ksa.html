Your key rights in figures

RightThe rule in briefBasis
End-of-service awardHalf a month's wage for each of the first five years and a full month for each year after, on the last actual wage, with part years pro rata.Article 84
Final settlementThe award by reason, leave pay (Art. 111), notice compensation (Art. 76) and outstanding pay, due within one or two weeks.Articles 84, 88, 111
Domestic workersA month's wage for every 4 consecutive years, two months' compensation for unlawful termination, and 30 days' leave after two years on renewal.Domestic Workers Regulation 1445 AH
Award on resignationNothing before two years, a third from two to five years, two thirds above five and below ten, and the full award from ten years.Article 85
Annual leave21 days a year, rising to 30 after five continuous years with the same employer.Article 109
Notice periodOpen-ended contracts on a monthly wage: 30 days if the worker ends it and 60 if the employer does; compensation is the wage for the notice period.Articles 75 and 76
Termination without a valid reason15 days' wage per year (open-ended) or the wage for the remaining term (fixed-term), with a minimum of two months' wage.Article 77
Sick leave30 days on full pay, then 60 at three-quarters, then 30 unpaid, within a year from the first sick leave.Article 117
Maternity leave12 weeks on full pay, 6 of them compulsory after the birth; fathers get 3 days within 7 days of the birth.Articles 151 and 113
Special leaveMarriage 5 days; death of a spouse, parent or child 5; a sibling 3; a newborn 3 within 7 days; Hajj 10–15 days; a widow's iddah 4 months and 10 days.Articles 113–116 and 160
OvertimeThe actual hourly wage plus 50% of the basic hourly wage, or time off in lieu with the worker's consent.Article 107
Dismissal without an award9 exhaustive cases in Article 80, provided the worker can object; the award is forfeited.Article 80
Leaving without notice7 cases in Article 81 where the worker may leave and keep the full award.Article 81
GOSI contributionsFor Saudi employees: 9% for pensions (rising for new entrants since July 2024) and 0.75% for SANED, on basic pay and housing, capped at SAR 45,000.Social Insurance Law
Employee costSalary plus the employer's GOSI share (11.75% for Saudis, 2% for expats), the expat levy and iqama, and the end-of-service provision.Social Insurance Law and Art. 40
PensionPrevious system: last 2 years' average × months ÷ 480. New (from 3 July 2024): 2.25% per year and age 65.Social Insurance Law
Retirement age and date65 for new members, 60 Hijri years for those not affected, 58 years 4 months to 65 for affected members; early retirement after 300–360 months.Council of Ministers Resolution 1022
Lump-sum compensationFor those without a pension: old system, last 24 months' average × 10% per month for the first 60 and 12% after; new system, total pension contributions revalued. Paid at the age, on disability or on death, not on resignation.Social Insurance Law and M/273
Work injury100% daily allowance during treatment, a 100% pension for total disability, a proportional pension from 50%, 60 times the notional pension below 50%, and 84 or 60 times for non-Saudis.Social Insurance Law M/273
Nitaqat countingA Saudi counts fully on SAR 4,000 or more and as half from 3,000; the threshold rises to 5,000 on 25 April 2027 and 6,000 on 25 April 2028.Decision 61706 and royal directive
SANED60% of the average wage for 3 months (up to 9,000), then 50% (up to 7,500) for up to 12 months; not paid on resignation.Social Insurance Law
New electricity billFrom 1 January 2027: 21 halalas up to 6,000 kWh and 33 above for homes, fixed fees of SAR 70–100 by breaker, and SAR 500 support for eligible people.Saudi Electricity Company
New water billFrom 1 January 2027: SAR 1 per m³ up to 15, then 2, 4 and 6, sewage at half, and fixed fees of SAR 20 + 10 for a meter up to 20 mm.National Water Company
Dependent fee and iqama renewalSAR 400 a month per dependent for the head of family; the employer pays the expat levy (800 or 700 a month), work permit and iqama fee.Cabinet decisions and Art. 40
Exit re-entry visaSingle SAR 200 for two months and 100 per extra month; multiple 500 for three months and 200 per month; extensions from abroad cost double.Visa fee decision
Finance deduction limitTotal instalments may not exceed a third of salary for employees and a quarter for retirees.SAMA instructions
Home financeTotal DBR 55% for incomes of SAR 15,000 or less and 65% above, finance up to 90% of a first home, admin fee 1% up to 5,000.Responsible lending principles

The figures above are for private-sector employees under the Labour Law. Every calculator cites its source and last review date. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
