Estimate your social security payment

Wife, children and others you support, of any age. Do not count yourself.
Salaries, self-employment income, retirement pension. Half is counted.
Rent and government benefits. Counted in full.

How the developed social security payment is calculated

  1. Family minimum = SAR 1,320 for the head of family or an independent applicant + SAR 660 for each dependent.
  2. Counted income = half of earned income (salaries, pensions and business income) + all unearned income (rent and benefits).
  3. Payment = family minimum − counted income, if the counted income is below the minimum.
Family sizeMinimum (no income)
Independent applicant or head of family alone1,320
Head of family and two dependents2,640
Head of family and four dependents3,960
Head of family and six dependents5,280 (affected by the 5,000 cap)

Examples:
Head of family with a wife and three children and a salary of 2,000: minimum 3,960, counted income 1,000, so the payment is SAR 2,960.
An independent widow with a pension of 1,800 and rent income of 300: minimum 1,320, counted income 900 + 300 = 1,200, so the payment is SAR 120.

Important conditions

  • The main home and one car are not counted as wealth; cash savings are added to income in a way whose details have not been published.
  • Signs of ineligibility: two or more cars for an independent applicant, more than 3 cars for a family, or more than two domestic workers.
  • Any change in income or family size must be reported within 15 days.

This calculator is an estimate; the final amount is set by the Ministry of Human Resources after reviewing the application on the social support and protection platform. For other Saudi support, see the Hafiz calculator and the SANED calculator.
