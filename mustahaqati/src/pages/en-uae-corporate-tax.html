How UAE corporate tax is calculated

Taxable incomeRate

Above AED 375,0009%

Example: taxable income AED 1,000,000 and revenue AED 5 million. Tax = 9% × (1,000,000 − 375,000) = AED 56,250, an effective 5.6% of income.
With revenue of AED 2.5 million and income of AED 600,000, Small Business Relief brings the tax to zero instead of AED 20,250.

Small Business Relief

  • Condition: revenue of no more than AED 3 million in the tax period and the previous periods.
  • Period: tax periods starting from 1 June 2023 and ending by 31 December 2029, after the August 2026 extension.
  • Excluded: Qualifying Free Zone Persons and members of multinational groups with consolidated revenue above AED 3.15 billion.
  • Effect: no tax, but the period's losses are not carried forward, and registration and filing still apply.
