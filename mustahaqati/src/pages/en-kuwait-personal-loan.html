Central Bank of Kuwait limits

  • Consumer loans: 25 times net salary, capped at KD 25,000, up to 5 years.
  • Housing finance: up to KD 70,000 over 15 years.
  • Deductions: 40% of net salary for employees, 30% for retirees.
  • Interest: discount rate + 3% at most.

Example: KD 10,000 at 5.5% reducing over 60 months on a KD 1,000 net salary.

The instalment is about KD 191 a month (19.1% of salary), within the limit. The loan complies.

These limits come from the published Central Bank instructions; banks may apply stricter terms. The rate you enter is an estimate, so ask your bank for the actual rate.
