The formula

Gratuity = last wage × (0.5 × years of service up to 3 + 1 × years beyond 3), where the wage is the basic wage plus any social allowance (Article 47). Part years count pro rata, and resignation does not reduce the gratuity. Bahrainis are covered by social insurance instead; see the Bahrain pension calculator. Prefer Arabic? Use the Arabic version.
