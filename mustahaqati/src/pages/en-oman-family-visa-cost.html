Work out your family residence cost

Family residence fees in Oman

ItemAmount (OMR)For and when
Family joining visa30Wife and children under 21, first time
Visa renewal30Each renewal
Family visa for parents30At the authority's discretion
Resident card5 for one year, 10 for two, 15 for threeAdults and children over 10
Medical test30Adults, each issue and renewal

Example: a wife and two children over 10, two-year cycle: visas 90, cards 30 and medical 30 = OMR 150, then OMR 75 a year. If the children are under 10, it is OMR 20 less.

A salary of at least OMR 150 has been required since February 2023. Resident card fees were cut in 2025 to 5, 10 and 15 from 6 and 11. For your net pay after contributions use the Oman net salary calculator.
