Notice rules in the UAE

  • Length: set by the contract, between 30 and 90 days, for both resignation and termination (Article 43).
  • Pay in lieu: whoever does not observe the notice pays the other side the wage for the notice period, or for the part not served, even without any loss.
  • Wage used: the last gross wage — basic plus allowances — divided by 30 for the daily rate.
  • Probation (Article 9): 14 days' notice from the employer; one month from an employee moving to another UAE employer; 14 days from an employee leaving the country.

Example: basic AED 10,000 and allowances AED 5,000, a 60-day notice period, and the employer ends the contract immediately.

Daily wage = 15,000 ÷ 30 = AED 500. Notice pay = 60 × 500 = AED 30,000, owed by the employer on top of gratuity and unused leave.
