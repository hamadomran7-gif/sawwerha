Widely reported changes that have not happened

  • Kuwait — 35 days of leave: a 2019 proposal that was never issued; 30 working days have applied since Law 85/2017.
  • UAE — longer private-sector maternity leave: under study since May 2025; the minimum is still 60 days.
  • Saudi Arabia — dependent fee change: the Ministry of Finance said in 2024 the fee was under review, but no decision was issued, so it remains SAR 400 a month per person.
  • Oman — savings scheme for non-Omanis: a 9% contribution replacing gratuity has not started yet; its start date is no later than 19 July 2027.
