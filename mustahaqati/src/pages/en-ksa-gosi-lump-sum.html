Work out your lump sum

Basic plus housing, capped at 45,000.

Which system applies to you?

Anyone with any uncompensated contribution period before 3 July 2024 stays under the old rules for the lump sum, even for months after that date. Anyone first insured from 3 July 2024 (or compensated for all earlier periods and then back at work) is under the new system.

ItemOld systemNew system
AmountAverage × (10% per month for the first 60 + 12% after)Total pension contributions of both parties, revalued by the consumer price index
When paidAt 60 Hijri after leaving work, or early for women, death, disability, loss of nationality and other casesAt 65 with under 180 months, or disability, death or loss of nationality
How paidOne paymentOne payment under 60 months; one payment or instalments from 60 months
AlternativeBuy up to 60 months at 18% to reach 120 months and a pension—

Examples:
Old system, 96 months on an average of 8,000: 8,000 × (6 + 36 × 0.12) = SAR 82,560 at 60 Hijri, or buy 24 months for 34,560 to get a pension of SAR 2,000 a month.
A woman under the old system who leaves work after 150 months on an average of 10,000: SAR 168,000, claimable as soon as she leaves.
New system, August 2024 to July 2027 on 8,000: 36 months of contributions (18%, then 19%, 20% and 21%) = SAR 54,960 as a minimum, paid at 65.

This is an estimate: the CPI revaluation and the number of instalments under the new system are set by the regulations, and the official amount is on your GOSI statement.
