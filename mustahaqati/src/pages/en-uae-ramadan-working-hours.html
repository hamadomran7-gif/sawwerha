Calculate Ramadan overtime

Basic only — overtime is calculated on it.

How long is the working day in Ramadan?

Article 17 of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021 leaves Ramadan hours to the executive regulations, and Article 15 of the regulations reduces normal working hours by two hours a day during the month. A worker on 8 hours works 6, and one on 9 hours works 7, with no cut in pay. Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to start around 8 February 2027 under the Umm al-Qura calendar, subject to the moon sighting.

What if the employer asks for a full day?

Article 19 treats anything beyond normal working hours as overtime. Because normal hours in Ramadan are the reduced ones, hours beyond them count as overtime at 25% above the basic hourly wage, or 50% between 10 pm and 4 am. This reading is drawn from the two provisions and supported by published legal opinions; no official circular states it expressly.

Example: basic salary AED 6,000, normal day 8 hours, and the employer asks for 8 hours throughout Ramadan (22 working days).

Ramadan day = 6 hours, so 2 extra hours a day.
Hourly wage = 6,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = AED 25.
Daily overtime = 2 × 25 × 1.25 = AED 62.50.
Total = 22 × 62.50 = AED 1,375. Dividing by 6 hours instead would give AED 1,833.33.
