Central Bank rules for personal loans

  • Maximum amount: 20 times the monthly salary or total income.
  • Term: no more than 48 months.
  • Debt burden: total instalments no more than 50% of salary, and 30% after retirement age.
  • Interest: calculated annually on the reducing balance and clearly disclosed.

Example: a loan of AED 150,000 at a 6.5% reducing rate over 48 months, on a salary of AED 12,000.

Instalment ≈ AED 3,557 a month, total interest ≈ AED 20,748.
The amount is within the limit (20 × 12,000 = 240,000), and the debt burden is 29.6%, under 50%. The loan complies with the rules.

Beware of flat rates

An advertised "3.99% flat" does not mean you pay 3.99%. A flat rate is charged on the whole original amount for the full term, even after you have repaid most of it, so it works out at roughly double the reducing rate.
