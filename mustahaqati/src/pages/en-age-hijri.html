The difference between Hijri and Gregorian age

The Gregorian year is solar, 365.25 days, and the Hijri year is lunar, 354.37 days. The gap of about 11 days a year adds up to a full year roughly every 33 years, so someone who is 33 in Gregorian years has completed 34 Hijri years.

The calculator uses the Umm al-Qura calendar to convert the date of birth, and works out the Hijri age from the actual number of days lived divided by the length of the lunar year.

When Hijri age matters

  • Government transactions in Saudi Arabia that use the Hijri date, such as the retirement age and some employment conditions.
  • Age in Islamic rulings.
  • Ages of majority and some procedures counted in Hijri years under certain regulations.
