Leave rules in Bahrain

  • Annual leave: 30 calendar days a year, earned at 2.5 days a month.
  • Pay: last basic salary plus social allowance (Article 47).
  • Sick leave: after three months — 15 days full pay, 20 half pay, 20 unpaid; unused paid days accumulate up to 240.

Example: basic plus social allowance BHD 1,000 and 20 unused days: 1,000 ÷ 30 × 20 = BHD 666.67.
Seven months of service: 7 × 2.5 = 17.5 days, worth BHD 583.33.
