A UAE national's home pays 7.5 fils in one slab with no surcharge. Industry pays 40 fils + 4, and government 23 fils up to 10,000 kWh and 38 above, + 5.

Worked examples

  • Expat flat, 1,500 kWh and 27 m³: electricity 1,500 × 0.23 = 345 plus a 75 surcharge, water 27 × 7.7 = 207.90 and VAT 31.40, a total of AED 659.30.
  • Expat villa, 7,000 kWh and 70 m³: electricity with surcharge 2,390, water 606.10 and VAT 149.81, a total of AED 3,145.91.
  • National's home, 6,000 kWh and 60 m³: electricity 450, water 198 and VAT 32.40, a total of AED 680.40.
