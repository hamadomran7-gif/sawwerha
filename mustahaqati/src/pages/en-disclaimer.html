What the content is

The calculators and explanations on this site are general guidance based on the legal texts published as of each page's last review date. They are not legal, financial or tax advice and do not create any professional relationship between us and you.

Why your actual figure may differ

  • Your contract may give you more than the legal minimum, in which case the contract applies.
  • Some sectors and free zones (such as DIFC and ADGM) have their own rules.
  • Official bodies and courts may interpret some provisions differently in particular cases.
  • Laws and rates may change after the last review date.

So always check with the official body (the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation in the UAE, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development in Saudi Arabia, the social insurance authority, or your bank) before making a decision.

Editorial policy and use of AI

We use AI tools to help draft explanations and organise pages. The formulas are coded from the legal text, the legal figures are kept in a single reference file that is reviewed by hand, and every calculator is tested against worked examples before publication. If you find a mistake, write to us and we will correct it.
