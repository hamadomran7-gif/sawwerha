How the bank sets your finance amount

The bank gives you the lowest of three amounts: what your salary allows after the debt burden ratio, what the loan-to-value ratio allows, and what you actually need after any support. The calculator shows all three and which one limits your finance.

In every band, personal finance instalments deducted from salary may not exceed 33.33% of it for employees and 25% for retirees.

Example: salary SAR 15,000 with a personal instalment of 2,000, a SAR 900,000 property, 25 years at 5%: the salary allows about 1,069,000 and the 90% LTV allows 810,000, so the finance is SAR 810,000 with an instalment of SAR 4,735 and a down payment of SAR 90,000.

Before you sign

  • Compare banks by APR, not the flat rate — the APR includes profit and all fees.
  • Admin fees are capped at 1% of the finance or SAR 5,000, whichever is lower, plus VAT.
  • The real estate transaction tax is 5%; the state covers it on the first million of a citizen's first home.
  • Banks usually use the lower of the price and the valuation, so finance may fall if the valuation comes in low.
