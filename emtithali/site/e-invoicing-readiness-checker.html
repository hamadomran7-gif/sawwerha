Frequently asked questions

Does UAE e-invoicing apply to small businesses?

Yes, if they deal with businesses or government entities. Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 31 March 2027 and go live from 1 July 2027.

What if all my sales are to individuals?

Sales to individual consumers are out of scope for now, and a business that only sells to individuals is not covered until a Ministerial Decision says otherwise. Any invoice you issue to a business or a government entity is in scope.

What is the penalty for appointing a provider late?

AED 5,000 when the deadline passes, then AED 5,000 for each further month or part of a month, up to AED 50,000 in any 12 consecutive months, under Cabinet Decision 173 of 2026.

Does the checker store or send my answers?

No. The checker runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not stored or sent anywhere, and they are gone when you close the page.
