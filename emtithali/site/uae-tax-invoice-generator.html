What a UAE tax invoice must show

Article 59 of the VAT Executive Regulation lists these particulars, and the generator puts all of them on a tax invoice:

  • The words “Tax Invoice”.
  • The supplier’s name, address and Tax Registration Number.
  • The recipient’s name and address, and its TRN where it is registered.
  • A sequential or unique invoice number that identifies the invoice and its order.
  • The date of issue, and the date of supply if it is different.
  • A description of the goods or services and, for each line, the unit price, quantity, VAT rate and amount payable in AED.
  • Any discount, the gross amount payable in AED, and the VAT amount in AED.

The generator charges VAT at the 5% standard rate or at zero, in AED only. It does not cover reverse-charge supplies, which need a specific statement on the invoice. The issuer remains responsible for the details.

When is a simplified tax invoice enough?

The same Article allows a simplified tax invoice where the recipient is not registered, or is registered and the consideration does not exceed AED 10,000, except under the reverse charge. The full invoice from this generator carries every particular of the simplified one.

Once e-invoicing starts

The document you download is a PDF, not an electronic invoice under the UAE e-invoicing system. For transactions in scope, invoices go through an Accredited Service Provider from your mandatory date. The generator stays useful for quotations and for transactions out of scope, such as sales to individuals.

Frequently asked questions

What must a UAE tax invoice include?

The words Tax Invoice; the supplier's name, address and TRN; the recipient's name, address and TRN where registered; an invoice number; the issue date and the date of supply if different; each line with its unit price, quantity, VAT rate and amount in AED; any discount; the total; and the VAT amount in AED.

When is a simplified tax invoice enough?

Where the recipient is not VAT-registered, or is registered and the consideration does not exceed AED 10,000, except under the reverse charge, according to Article 59 of the VAT Executive Regulation.

Will a PDF invoice still be accepted after e-invoicing starts?

Not for transactions in scope. From 1 July 2027, businesses with revenue below AED 50 million issue invoices to businesses and government entities through an Accredited Service Provider, and a PDF file is not an electronic invoice.

Does the generator keep my invoice data?

No. It runs in your browser, nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and the PDF is created on your device.
