Accreditation status and complaints

The Ministry of Finance’s e-invoicing page (Arabic) says the status of accredited service providers has been updated to full accreditation, and it links a card for complaints about an accredited e-invoicing service provider to Tawasul 171, the government platform for messages, case tracking and the 171 hotline. We read both pages on 26 November 2026.

What Emtithali is

Emtithali is an independent, Arabic-language platform in a pre-launch trial. It is not part of the Ministry of Finance or the Federal Tax Authority, and it is not an Accredited Service Provider.

It helps businesses with revenue below AED 50 million compare accredited providers before their deadline. Under Article 5(1)(b) of Ministerial Decision 244 of 2025, these businesses must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 31 March 2027 and implement the Electronic Invoicing System by 1 July 2027.

How Emtithali earns

The service is free for businesses. Some providers may pay us a commission if a business signs with them, and the business pays nothing extra. This has no effect on whether a provider is listed here or on its position: the table follows the Ministry’s order.

For listed providers

If anything about your company here differs from the Ministry’s list, reply to the message you received from Emtithali, or write to the contact address at the bottom of our homepage, and we will correct it. We copy only the company name, accreditation number and website from the Ministry’s list.

Frequently asked questions

How many eInvoicing Accredited Service Providers are there in the UAE?

90 on the Ministry of Finance list, as we read it on 27 November 2026. The Ministry says it updates the list periodically to include newly accredited providers.

When must a business with revenue below AED 50 million appoint an Accredited Service Provider?

By 31 March 2027, and it must implement the Electronic Invoicing System by 1 July 2027, under Article 5(1)(b) of Ministerial Decision 244 of 2025.

Is Emtithali part of the Ministry of Finance, or an Accredited Service Provider?

No. Emtithali is an independent, non-government platform in Arabic. It is not part of the Ministry of Finance or the Federal Tax Authority, and it is not an Accredited Service Provider.
