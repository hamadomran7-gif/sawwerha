FAQ

Is the product itself AI-generated?

No. We cut out your real product from your photo and keep its pixels unchanged — shape, colour and label text. AI only generates the background and scene around it. The white-background main image is your real photo, cleaned.

Will noon or Amazon accept the images?

Nobody can guarantee a marketplace review decision, but our white images follow the published main-image rules: pure white background, the whole product, no text, logos, borders or watermarks. Package images include an Amazon version (product fills 85% of the frame) and a noon version (about 80% with 10% margins). If an image is rejected because of our edit, we redo it as your free revision.

How fast is delivery?

The free sample usually arrives within 2–6 hours. Paid packages are delivered within 24 hours of receiving your photos, with a full refund if not delivered within 48 hours of payment.

How do I pay?

Upfront through a secure Ziina payment link sent in the WhatsApp chat. Prices are in AED and final.

Do you shoot clothing on models?

No. We don't use AI-generated models. Clothing works as flat-lay or detail shots; for apparel that marketplaces require on a model, a studio is the better choice.
